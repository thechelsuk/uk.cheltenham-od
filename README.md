
# Cheltenham Open Data

A site dedicated to the collection and dissemination of local open data in the Cheltenham and Gloucestershire area in the UK. Pages are built from official and public sources (ONS/Nomis, BODS, Ofcom, DfE, GOV.UK, OpenStreetMap and more) and refreshed automatically on a schedule.

## What's on the Site

**Transport & getting around**
- Cheapest local fuel prices, updated daily
- Bus routes serving Cheltenham and their BODS publishing status, plus live disruptions
- EV charging point locations
- Broadband and internet speed coverage by ward

**Parcels & post**
- Post Office locations
- InPost self-service locker locations
- DPD Pickup points (shops, newsagents and kiosks that accept parcel drop-offs)

**Local life & services**
- Schools — primary, secondary, independent and special
- GP practices and pharmacies
- Public toilets
- Recycling banks, centres and a recycling FAQ
- Points of interest, museums, historic sites and blue plaques
- Third spaces and community hubs
- Places to stay, including a dedicated Cheltenham Festival accommodation guide
- Local classified adverts, jobs board and community notices
- Local events and festivals

**Civic & environmental data**
- Local planning applications
- Food hygiene ratings
- Street-level crime data by neighbourhood
- Flood warnings and alerts
- Air quality readings
- Fix My Street issue reports
- Security alerts (MI5 threat level tracking)
- Confirmed public holidays

**Community support**
- Local food banks and their current needs
- Helplines and support contact numbers

**Cheltenham facts & figures**
- Population, growth, employment and Census 2021 statistics, sourced from ONS/Nomis
- Full employment rate history since 2004
- A short history timeline of the town
- House prices from Land Registry and ONS

**Weather**
- 10-day weather forecast for Cheltenham

**News**
- Local news aggregated and updated hourly

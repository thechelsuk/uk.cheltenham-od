Cheltenham has two free, timed 2k junior parkruns for 4 to 14 year olds,
both starting at 9am every Sunday. They are friendly, volunteer-led events
where children can run, jog or walk at their own pace.

## When and Where

- **Cheltenham junior parkrun:** Sundays at 9am in The Park, GL50 2RH.
- **Burrow's Field junior parkrun:** Sundays at 9am at Burrow's Field,
  Moorend Grove, GL53 0HA.

## Before Your First Run

Register once, for free, on the [parkrun site](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/register/)
and print your barcode or save it on your phone. It is needed to get a time,
and it works at any junior parkrun event.

See the [Cheltenham junior parkrun](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/cheltenham-juniors/) and
[Burrow's Field junior parkrun](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/burrowsfield-juniors/)
event pages for the course maps and latest news, or the
[map of every parkrun in Cheltenham](/cheltenham-sports/parkrun).

## Good For

- Children aged 4 to 14, whether they are new to running or already keen
- Families looking for a free Sunday morning activity
- Building confidence and staying active in a friendly, non-competitive setting

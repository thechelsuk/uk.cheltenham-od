Opiuml is a podcast player built around two lists: an Inbox for everything
new, and a Queue for what you actually want to hear. New episodes land in
your Inbox, or go straight to the top or bottom of your Queue, and the rest
quietly tidy themselves away.

## What It Does

- Triage new episodes with a swipe: queue next, queue last, or archive
- Rules for each podcast: where new episodes go, how many to keep, playback
  speed and skip intervals
- Keep only the latest, so a daily news show keeps just today's episode
- Chapters, artwork and show notes, with chapter skipping on the lock screen
- Trim silence, voice boost, volume levelling and a sleep timer
- Video podcasts, YouTube channels (through their public feeds) and your own
  audio files and audiobooks
- Apple Watch, CarPlay, widgets and Siri Shortcuts
- Import from any podcast app with OPML, plus listening history and stats

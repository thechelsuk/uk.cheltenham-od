Lansdown Foundry lets you design your own font with Apple Pencil, then use it
in every app. It turns your strokes into a real, installable typeface, with
the thick-and-thin of a pen, kerning and accents, in Regular, Bold and Italic.
The built-in stencils are named after Cheltenham places: Suffolks,
Battledown and Leckhampton.

## What It Does

- Start from a stencil (a book serif, a rounded sans or a slab serif) or from
  a blank page
- Draw with a calligraphic nib that gets heavier where you press
- Draw a few key letters and the rest follow: the n gives you h, m and u, and
  accented letters come along for free
- Add balls, curls and swashes to the end of any stroke
- Spacing and kerning measured from your letters, with Bold, Italic and Bold
  Italic made for you
- Install your font on your iPad or Mac for Pages, Keynote, Procreate and
  more, or export TrueType, WOFF and WOFF2 for the web
- Your fonts stay in step between iPad and Mac through iCloud Drive

## Good For

- Turning your own handwriting into a font
- Letterers and calligraphers who want their work as a typeface
- Makers selling fonts on Etsy, Gumroad or Creative Market

You'll need an iPad with Apple Pencil, or a Mac with Apple silicon, where you

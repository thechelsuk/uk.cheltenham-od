OPMLMan is an OPML and RSS feed manager. Open the OPML file from your feed
reader or podcast app, tap Check, and it tells you which feeds are active,
which have gone quiet, which have moved and which are gone for good.

## What It Does

- Checks every feed when you tap Check, and never in the background
- Shows each feed's latest item, how often it publishes and what the server
  said
- Marks feeds as active, slowing, dormant, moved, not found or failing
- Finds duplicates: the same feed over http and https, or one address
  redirecting to another
- Merges lists, or splits one into podcasts, video and articles
- Exports clean OPML per collection, combined, or one file per type

- No account and no analytics: it only talks to the feeds you check

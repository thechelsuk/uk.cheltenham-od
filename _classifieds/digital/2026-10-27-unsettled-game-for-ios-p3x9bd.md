There are no instructions. There is no way back. Unsettled is a puzzle game
disguised as an ordinary-looking phone: the screen is blank and a mail icon
has one unread message. Everything you need is already in front of you, if
you stop looking at what's in front of you.

## What It Does

- Each message is a puzzle: read it, read between the lines, and scroll to
  the bottom
- Every answer is a number, typed on the keypad
- The controls on screen are real, but most of them are decoys
- Some puzzles use the phone itself, and some don't live in the app at all
- Three chapters and thirty-five locks, with sealed messages that only open
  once you've found every number
- Chapter two arrives a day later, in real time

## How Much It Costs

- **Free:** chapters one and two
- **Chapter 3:** £2.99, a one-off purchase with fifteen harder locks and the
  truth about who has been writing to you
- **Field Training:** £1.99, daily drills once the story is finished
- **3 Hints:** £0.99. There's no free hint, but every five levels earns you
  one

## Why You'll Like It

## Good For

- Escape room fans looking for something to play at home

Nuchronic helps people living with ME/CFS, long COVID and other chronic
illnesses keep track of their energy and symptoms in seconds, and take a
clear record to their appointments. It's quick to use on a bad day, calm to
look at, and keeps everything on your phone.

## What It Does

- Check in with a few taps: energy, symptoms, PEM or crash, sleep, pain,
  brain fog and dizziness, each on a simple 0 to 4 scale
- Add up to four symptoms of your own, or tap "Same as yesterday"
- No streaks and no nagging: a missed day is just blank
- A year in pixels and month summaries, so patterns are easy to see
- Write a crash plan on a good day, and open it in large, calm text when
  thinking is hard
- Keep your health summary, medications, appointments and care contacts
  together
- Short, plain-English guides to pacing, PEM and boom and bust, with links to
  the NHS, NICE and the CDC

## How Much It Costs

- **Free:** check-ins, history, crash plan, records and guides
- **Nuchronic Plus:** £4.99, a one-off purchase with no subscription. It turns
  your records into a PDF for your GP or clinic, in A4 or US Letter

## Why You'll Like It

- Private by design: no account, no tracking, and nothing leaves your phone
  unless you choose to share it
- Large tap targets, support for larger text and VoiceOver
- Made in the UK

## Good For

- People living with ME/CFS, long COVID, fibromyalgia, POTS or other chronic
  illnesses
- Anyone learning to pace and wanting to spot their own patterns
- Getting the most out of a short GP or specialist appointment
- Carers helping someone keep their records in one place

Nuchronic keeps your own records and plans. It isn't medical advice and never
interprets what you enter. If your symptoms change or worry you, contact your
GP, or NHS 111.

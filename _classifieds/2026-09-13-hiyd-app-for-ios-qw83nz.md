Hiyd is the note-taking companion built for Jekyll bloggers — write your post,
fill in the front matter, and publish straight to GitHub without ever
touching a terminal.

## What It Does

- Writes valid Markdown with front matter, ready to drop into any Jekyll blog
- Publishes directly to GitHub using a personal access token — no Git client
  needed
- Syncs files via iCloud and exports to apps like Working Copy
- Configure your own custom front matter key/value pairs
- Full-text search across your whole archive, with live Markdown preview
- Shortcuts and App Intents support for automating your writing workflow

## Why You'll like It

- Every feature is free to use — no paywalled functionality, ever
- Customisable app appearance to match how you like to write
- Optional tips and a Supporter subscription if you want to say thanks

## Good For

- Jekyll bloggers who want to publish from their phone
- Writers who'd rather skip the terminal and Git commands
- Anyone drafting posts on the go and syncing them home via iCloud

Download Hiyd today and take your Jekyll blog with you wherever you write.

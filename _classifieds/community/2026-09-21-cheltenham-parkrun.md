Cheltenham has two free, timed 5k parkruns, both starting at 9am every
Saturday. They are friendly, volunteer-led events for all ages and abilities,
so walkers, joggers and runners are all welcome.

## When and Where

- **Cheltenham parkrun:** Saturdays at 9am in Pittville Park, GL50 4SH. The
  start is by the pavilion on the cycle path, next to the lake and Leisure at
  Cheltenham.
- **King George V Playing Field parkrun:** Saturdays at 9am at King George V
  Playing Field, Brooklyn Road, GL51 8DZ. Parking is at Coronation Square,
  GL51 7RG.

## Before Your First Run

Register once, for free, on the [parkrun site](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/register/)
and print your barcode or save it on your phone. You need it to get a time,
and it works at any parkrun event.

See the [Cheltenham parkrun](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/cheltenham/) and
[King George V Playing Field parkrun](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/kinggeorgevplayingfield/)
event pages for the course maps and latest news, or the
[map of every parkrun in Cheltenham](/cheltenham-sports/parkrun).

## Good For

- Runners, joggers and walkers of every pace
- Families, friends and anyone new to running
- A free, regular way to meet people and start the weekend

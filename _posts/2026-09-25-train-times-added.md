Cheltenham Open Data has published a new [train times page](/cheltenham-train-times), showing the next departures and arrivals at Cheltenham Spa station.

For each train it lists the scheduled and expected times, the platform, the operator and whether it is on time, delayed or cancelled. Departures also show the stations each train calls at, so you can check your stop before you set off. Any notices for the station appear at the top of the page. Further down, the station usage figures show how many passengers use Cheltenham Spa each year.

This joins [roadworks](/cheltenham-roadworks), [car parks](/cheltenham-car-parks), [cycle routes](/cheltenham-cycle-routes) and [bus data](/cheltenham-bus-data) under Getting Around.

## FAQs

### What Does the Train Times Page Show?

- The next 10 departures and next 10 arrivals at Cheltenham Spa, with scheduled and expected times, platforms, operators and delay or cancellation reasons where given. Past trains aren't shown.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- Train times come from National Rail Enquiries' live departure boards. Annual passenger numbers come from the [Office of Rail and Road](https://dataportal.orr.gov.uk/statistics/usage/estimates-of-station-usage).

### How Often Is It Updated?

- Regularly throughout the day, and the time of the latest update is shown on the page. The passenger figures are annual, so they change once a year.

### What If No Trains Are Showing?

- Each section points to National Rail's own [departures](https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/live-trains/departures/cheltenham-spa/) and [arrivals](https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/live-trains/arrivals/cheltenham-spa/) pages for Cheltenham Spa. Delays and platform changes can happen at short notice, so it is always worth checking the station screens when you arrive.

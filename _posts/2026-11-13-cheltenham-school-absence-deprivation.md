Cheltenham Open Data compared pupil absence at each of the 28 state primary schools in Cheltenham borough
with the deprivation of the ward the school is in. The seven primaries in the town's five most deprived wards
had the seven highest persistent absence rates, all above the Gloucestershire primary average of 11.9%, while
Ofsted rated almost every school Good, in the richest and poorest wards alike. Every school's figures are on the
[Cheltenham school absence page](/cheltenham-schools/absence).

## Key Figures

- **22.1%** of pupils at the seven primaries in the five most deprived wards (Oakley, St Mark's, St Paul's,
  Hesters Way and Springbank) were persistently absent, missing at least one session in ten.
- **5.3%** at the nine primaries in the five least deprived wards with a primary school (Benhall, the Reddings
  and Fiddler's Green, Charlton Kings, Prestbury, Warden Hill and Leckhampton).
- **7.2% against 3.5%:** the share of all sessions missed in the same two groups.
- **15.1%:** the lowest persistent absence rate of any primary in the five most deprived wards, higher than
  every primary elsewhere in the borough.
- **25 of 28** primaries were rated Good by Ofsted at the end of the school year.

## Absence Follows Deprivation

Ranked by the deprivation of their ward, the schools fall into two clear groups. The highest rate was at
[Oakwood Primary School](/cheltenham-schools/oakwood-primary-school) in Oakley, Cheltenham's most deprived ward,
where 35.7% of pupils were persistently absent. Next were [Hesters Way Primary School](/cheltenham-schools/hesters-way-primary-school),
which is in Springbank ward, at 27.4%, and [Gardners Lane Primary School](/cheltenham-schools/gardners-lane-primary-school)
in St Paul's, at 25.1%.

At the other end, [Warden Hill Primary School](/cheltenham-schools/warden-hill-primary-school),
[Charlton Kings Infants' School](/cheltenham-schools/charlton-kings-infants-school) and
[Leckhampton Church of England Primary School](/cheltenham-schools/leckhampton-church-of-england-primary-school)
had the lowest rates, at 2.7%, 2.7% and 3.1%.

It's the same divide our [Two Cheltenhams](/news/cheltenham-deprivation-two-cheltenhams) analysis found in
the government's deprivation rankings, where neighbourhoods half a mile apart sit at opposite ends of England's
league table. The Department for Education's own [national figures](https://explore-education-statistics.service.gov.uk/find-statistics/pupil-absence-in-schools-in-england/2024-25#d5e6-free-school-meals-fsm-eligibility)
show the same pattern for individual pupils: those eligible for free school meals are more than two and a half
times as likely to be persistently absent as those who are not.

## Ofsted Grades Don't Split the Same Way

Six of the seven primaries in the most deprived wards were rated Good, the same grade as every primary in the
least deprived wards. The two Outstanding schools, [Christ Church CofE Primary School](/cheltenham-schools/christ-church-cofe-primary-school)
and [Gloucester Road Primary School](/cheltenham-schools/gloucester-road-primary-school), are both in St Peter's,
the sixth most deprived ward, and both had persistent absence below the county average.

Two grades changed during the school year. [Naunton Park Primary School](/cheltenham-schools/naunton-park-primary-school)
in Leckhampton went from Outstanding to Good in October 2024, and [Holy Trinity Church of England Primary School](/cheltenham-schools/holy-trinity-church-of-england-primary-school)
in Pittville went from Requires improvement to Good in June 2025. Each school's full inspection history is on
its own page.

## Notes to Editors

- **Method:** Each school is placed in the ward that contains its site. Wards are ranked from 1, the most
  deprived, to 20 by their average Index of Multiple Deprivation 2025 score, as in our
  [Cheltenham wards](/cheltenham-wards) figures. Group rates are weighted by pupil enrolments, not averages of
  school rates. Persistent absence is missing 10% or more of possible sessions. Ofsted grades are the overall
  grade in force at the start and end of the 2024/25 school year. For inspections from September 2024, which
  graded each area but gave no overall grade, the quality of education grade is used; ungraded inspections keep
  the previous grade.
- **Sources:** Department for Education [Pupil absence in schools in England](https://explore-education-statistics.service.gov.uk/find-statistics/pupil-absence-in-schools-in-england/2024-25),
  academic year 2024/25; Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government English Indices of Deprivation
  2025; Ofsted inspection outcomes.
- **Caveats:** The ward a school is in is not where all its pupils live. These are 28 schools, and the figures
  show a pattern across them, not why any pupil misses school or what drives a school's grade. Secondary schools
  are left out because pupils travel from across the area to reach them, and special schools are left out
  because their pupils' needs are very different. Schools in the wider GL50 to GL54 area but outside Cheltenham
  borough are not included.
- **Data and updates:** The school absence page updates when the Department for Education publishes new figures,
  each March for the full school year and each October for the autumn and spring terms. For questions or
  interviews, use the [contact page](/contact).

## FAQs

### Which Cheltenham Primary School Has the Highest Absence?

- Oakwood Primary School in Oakley, where 35.7% of pupils were persistently absent in 2024/25 and 9.8% of
  sessions were missed.

### Do Schools With Better Ofsted Grades Have Lower Absence?

- Not in a way these figures can show. 25 of Cheltenham's 28 state primaries were rated Good at the end of
  2024/25, and their absence rates ranged from 2.7% to 27.4% of pupils persistently absent.

### What Is Persistent Absence?

- A pupil is persistently absent if they miss 10% or more of their morning and afternoon sessions, for any
  reason, including illness.

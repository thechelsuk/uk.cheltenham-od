Filling up at the motorway costs a lot more than filling up in town. Using prices that stations reported to the government's fuel price scheme in the ten days to 6 October 2026, the two forecourts at Strensham services on the M5 were charging 195.9p and 198.9p a litre for unleaded. The cheapest supermarket within 20 miles was 167.7p.

That's a 31p gap: about **£17 on a 55-litre tank**.

Our [fuel prices page](/cheltenham-fuel-prices) updates every hour, so check it before you set off.

## FAQs

### How Much Do Prices Vary Inside Cheltenham?

- Much less than you might think. Within three miles of the town centre, unleaded ran from 169.7p to 173.9p a litre, a difference of about £2.30 on a 55-litre tank.

### Are Supermarkets Cheaper?

- Usually. Across stations within 20 miles of Cheltenham, the middle supermarket price for unleaded was 170.9p a litre, against 174.9p at other forecourts, about £2.20 a tank.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- From the GOV.UK fuel price scheme, which requires larger retailers to publish their prices, under the Open Government Licence. We ignore prices more than ten days old, so a station that hasn't reported recently won't show up as the cheapest.

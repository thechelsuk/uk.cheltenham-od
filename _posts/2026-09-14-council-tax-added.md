Cheltenham Open Data has published a new [council tax page](/cheltenham-council-tax), showing charges by band (A-H) for every area of the borough for the current tax year.

Five of Cheltenham's parishes — Charlton Kings, Leckhampton with Warden Hill, Prestbury, Swindon Village and Up Hatherley — set their own additional precept, so pay a little more than the rest of the borough. The page also links straight to the official GOV.UK service for checking which band your own property is in.

## FAQs

### What Does the Council Tax Page Show?

- A table of council tax charges by band (A-H) for each of Cheltenham's five precepting parishes, plus the standard rate for the rest of the borough.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- Cheltenham Borough Council's own published charges. There's no CSV or open data file for this — it's scraped from the council's website, since that's the only source that exists.

### How Do I Find My Property's Band?

- Use the official [GOV.UK council tax band checker](https://www.gov.uk/council-tax-bands), run by the Valuation Office Agency — the same service linked from the top of the page.

### How Often Is It Updated?

- Monthly, though the figures themselves only change once a year in April. The "last updated" date is at the bottom of the page.

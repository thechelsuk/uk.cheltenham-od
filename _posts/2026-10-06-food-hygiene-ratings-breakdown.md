Cheltenham's restaurants, cafés, takeaways and shops score well on food hygiene. Of the 1,020 food businesses in the town with a current rating, **913 (about 90%) have the top rating of 5**, "very good".

Our [food hygiene page](/cheltenham-food-standards) lists every business and its latest rating, with a [list of five-rated places](/cheltenham-food-standards/rated-five).

## FAQs

### How Do the Ratings Break Down?

| Rating | Meaning | Businesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | Very good | 913 |
| 4 | Good | 88 |
| 3 | Generally satisfactory | 15 |
| 2 | Improvement necessary | 1 |
| 1 | Major improvement necessary | 3 |
| 0 | Urgent improvement necessary | 0 |

- Another 33 businesses are waiting for their first inspection, and 32 are exempt, usually because the food risk is very low.

### What Does a Rating Measure?

- Inspectors from Cheltenham Borough Council check how food is handled, stored and prepared, the condition of the premises, and how the business manages food safety. A rating isn't a guide to the food's quality or taste.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- From the Food Standards Agency's published ratings, under the Open Government Licence. Figures are as of 6 October 2026, and the page refreshes as new inspections are published.

Cheltenham Open Data analysed the latest food hygiene rating of every food business in Cheltenham with a current rating. The town scores well: 913 of 1,020 rated businesses (about 90%) have the top rating of 5, "very good", and just four are rated 1 or 2. None is rated 0. Our [food hygiene page](/cheltenham-food-standards) lists every business and its latest rating, with a [list of five-rated places](/cheltenham-food-standards/rated-five).

## Key Figures

- **913 of 1,020** rated food businesses have a rating of 5.
- **4** are rated 1 or 2, needing improvement, and none is rated 0.
- **75.8%** of takeaways and sandwich shops are rated 5 (72 of 95), the lowest of any type.
- **77.3%** of other shops, not counting supermarkets, are rated 5 (99 of 128).
- **1,001 of 1,020** ratings were given in 2024 or later.

## By Type of Business

Places that care for people score highest. Of 82 hospitals, childcare and care settings, 81 are rated 5, as are 63 of 64 schools, colleges and university sites, and all 48 mobile caterers. Restaurants, cafés and canteens are close to the town average at 89.3% (276 of 309), as are pubs, bars and nightclubs at 90.1% (82 of 91).

Takeaways and smaller shops are the exception. About three in four are rated 5, and most of the rest are rated 4, "good". Of the four businesses rated 1 or 2, two are restaurants or cafés, one is a supermarket and one is another shop.

## Notes to Editors

- **Method:** Every business in Cheltenham with a current food hygiene rating from 0 to 5, grouped by the Food Standards Agency's own business types. Shares by type are shown for types with at least 20 rated businesses. Businesses awaiting inspection or exempt are left out of the shares.
- **Source:** Food Standards Agency food hygiene rating data, from inspections carried out by Cheltenham Borough Council.
- **Caveats:** A rating reflects the business on the day of its last inspection. Businesses can ask for a revisit after making improvements, so a low rating may already be under review. Ratings measure hygiene and food safety management, not the quality or taste of the food.
- **Data and updates:** The food hygiene page refreshes as new inspections are published. For questions or interviews, use the [contact page](/contact).

## FAQs

### What Does a Rating Measure?

- Inspectors from Cheltenham Borough Council check how food is handled, stored and prepared, the condition of the premises, and how the business manages food safety. A rating isn't a guide to the food's quality or taste.

### Which Cheltenham Food Businesses Score Lowest?

- Takeaways and sandwich shops, where 75.8% are rated 5, and smaller shops other than supermarkets, at 77.3%. Most of the rest in both groups are rated 4, "good".

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- From the Food Standards Agency's published ratings, under the Open Government Licence. Figures are as of 2026-10-06, and the food hygiene page refreshes as new inspections are published.

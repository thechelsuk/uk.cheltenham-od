Cheltenham Open Data has published a new [street works page](/cheltenham-street-works), covering the works on the town's own streets.

Until now, the site's [roadworks page](/cheltenham-roadworks) covered the M5, A417, A40 and A46, which are run by National Highways. The new page covers the local roads looked after by Gloucestershire County Council, where water, gas, electricity and broadband companies dig up the road and the council carries out repairs. It lists every works within 6 miles of the town centre on a map and in a table, with the traffic lights or road closures each one needs and when it starts and finishes.

## FAQs

### What Does the Street Works Page Show?

- Current and upcoming street works on local roads within 6 miles of Cheltenham town centre, on a map and in a table you can sort by street, start date or distance.
- Each works shows whether it was planned or unplanned, its status, the traffic management in place and who is carrying it out.

### Does It Include Emergency Repairs?

- Yes. Urgent and emergency works, such as burst water mains and gas leaks, are listed as unplanned works as soon as they are reported, alongside works booked weeks ahead.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- [Street Manager](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/find-and-use-roadworks-data), the Department for Transport's service that councils and utility companies use to plan and record street works in England, published under the Open Government Licence.

### How Often Is It Updated?

- New works and changes are sent to the site as they happen, and the page is updated every two hours.

### What About Motorways and Major Roads?

- Works and closures on the M5, A417, A40 and A46 are on the [roadworks page](/cheltenham-roadworks), along with unplanned lane closures on those roads.

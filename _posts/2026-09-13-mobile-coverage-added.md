Cheltenham Open Data has added a [mobile phone coverage page](/cheltenham-mobile-coverage), a companion to the [broadband coverage page](/cheltenham-broadband-internet-speeds).

It covers every generation of mobile network from 2G through to 5G Standalone, broken down by whether you're indoors, outdoors, or just somewhere in the local area — plus a table showing how much of Cheltenham has a genuine choice of all four networks (EE, O2, Three and Vodafone), not just one.

Unlike the broadband page, this one isn't mapped ward by ward. Ofcom only publishes mobile coverage data down to constituency level, so every figure is a single average for the whole of Cheltenham — see the FAQs on the page for why.

## FAQs

### How Does This Relate to the Broadband Page?

- The broadband page shows home fixed-line speeds, mapped ward by ward. This one shows mobile phone signal — 4G, 5G and voice calls — for the whole Cheltenham constituency, since that's the finest level Ofcom publishes.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- Ofcom's Connected Nations programme, using coverage predictions submitted by the four mobile network operators. Refreshed roughly twice a year.

### Is 5G Coverage Any Good in Cheltenham?

- Ordinary 5G is strong outdoors from at least one network, but the newer 5G Standalone technology still has real gaps once you look for a strong signal — see the full breakdown on the page.

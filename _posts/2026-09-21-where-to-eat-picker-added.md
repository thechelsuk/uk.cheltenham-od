The [where to eat page](/cheltenham-where-to-eat) now has a button for when nobody can decide.

Select **Pick a place** and it chooses a restaurant, café, pub, bar or takeaway at random from those with a food hygiene rating of 4 or 5. You can limit it to restaurants and cafés, pubs and bars, or takeaways, and pick again as often as you like. Each result shows its rating, address, a link to its official rating record and directions, and a **Show on map** button that jumps to its pin.

The choice is made in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. It is random rather than a recommendation, and it can't know opening hours, so check them before you go.

## FAQs

### How Does the Random Pick Work?

- It chooses at random from the venues on the page with a hygiene rating of 4 or 5, optionally limited to a type of place.

### Can a Venue Pay to Be Picked?

- No. Every venue in the pool has the same chance.

### Does It Know Whether a Place Is Open?

- No. The data has no opening hours, so check the venue's own site or phone line before you go.

Cheltenham Open Data has added an ["All About Cheltenham" page](/about-cheltenham) to the site.

It's the town's key statistics in one place — population and growth back to 2001, current employment rates, and a Census 2021 headline figure — sourced directly from the ONS's [Nomis](https://www.nomisweb.co.uk/) service rather than a third-party aggregator, plus a short history timeline from the Domesday Book to today.

Cheltenham's population has grown from **110,024** in 2001 to **121,452** in 2025, a **10.4%** increase, with an employment rate of **79.3%**.

## FAQs

### What's on the Page?

- A population growth chart and table since 2001, current employment/economic activity/unemployment rates, a Census 2021 population figure as a cross-check, and a short timeline of notable dates in Cheltenham's history.

### How Much Has Cheltenham's Population Grown?

- From **110,024** in 2001 to **121,452** in 2025 — a **10.4%** increase over 24 years, according to the ONS's mid-year population estimates.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- Nomis, the ONS's official service for labour market and census data — the same source behind the government's own local area profiles, not a third-party estimate.

### Is There More Detail on Employment?

- Yes — a [full employment rate history since 2004](/cheltenham-employment-history) covers economic activity, employment and unemployment rates year by year, with a chart.

### How Often Is This Updated?

- Monthly, matching how often this kind of data actually changes — annual population estimates and quarterly employment figures don't need a faster refresh.

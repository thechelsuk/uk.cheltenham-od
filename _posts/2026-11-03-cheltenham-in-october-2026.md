October in Cheltenham's open data brought 13 new food hygiene ratings, 333 finished street works, 42 power cuts and the most sewage spills in a month for at least a year.

## Food Hygiene Ratings

Thirteen businesses had a new [food hygiene rating](/cheltenham-food-standards) published by the Food Standards Agency in October.

The biggest change was at Morrisons on Caernarvon Road, which went from a rating of 1 to 5 after an inspection on 12 October. Three businesses already on the register had a new rating:

| Business   | Postcode | Was | Now | Inspected  |
| ---------- | -------- | --: | --: | ---------- |
| Morrisons  | GL51 3BW |   1 |   5 | 2026-10-12 |
| Rainbowls  | GL52 6AR |   4 |   5 | 2026-10-14 |
| Rogan Josh | GL52 2LX |   4 |   3 | 2026-09-12 |

{% include key.html text="**Was** previous rating, **Now** new rating. Ratings run from 0 to 5, where 5 is very good." %}

Ten businesses were rated for the first time, all with a 5: Crofton Springs, Ghurka Kitchen, Humble Pies Pizzas, John Lewis, KFC on the High Street, Kings Larder, Pacheco Paellas, Platter by Benugo at John Lewis, Siren Cheltenham Bar and The Great Plate CIC.

Ten businesses joined the register, and eighteen left it, usually because they have closed or changed hands.

## Planning Applications

Cheltenham Borough Council has so far registered [46 planning applications](/cheltenham-planning-applications) received in October. More will be added as the council checks them. Twenty-nine were for work on trees, and seven were full planning applications, including:

- three new homes at Atherstone, 17 Church Road, St Marks
- refurbishing and slightly extending McDonald's at Kingsditch Retail Park

## Finished Street Works

Between 11 and 31 October, [333 street works](/cheltenham-street-works) within 6 miles of Cheltenham finished. Twenty-seven of them needed a road closure, and 91 were urgent or emergency works.

| Who did the work               | Works |
| ------------------------------ | ----: |
| Severn Trent Water             |   154 |
| BT                             |    60 |
| Gloucestershire County Council |    44 |
| National Grid Electricity      |    36 |
| Virgin Media                   |    11 |

Tewkesbury Road and Gloucester Road had the most, with 11 each. The longest road closure to finish was Severn Trent's work on Upper Bath Street, from 28 September to 16 October.

## Power Cuts

National Grid recorded [42 power cuts](/cheltenham-power-cuts) across GL50 to GL54 postcodes in October, 29 of them unplanned, affecting 9,843 homes and businesses in total. The largest in Cheltenham itself was on 21 October around GL51 4XA, when 2,035 homes and businesses lost power.

## Fuel Prices

[Fuel prices](/cheltenham-fuel-prices) at Cheltenham's forecourts eased slightly over October, after rising through September. The average price of unleaded fell from 174.5p a litre on 1 October to 171.6p on 31 October, and diesel from 199.7p to 198.2p.

| Fuel     | September | October | Cheapest in October |
| -------- | --------: | ------: | ------------------: |
| Unleaded |    170.9p |  173.5p |              165.7p |
| Diesel   |    194.3p |  198.7p |              187.7p |

Across the month, Cheltenham was about a penny a litre cheaper than the UK average, which was 174.7p for unleaded and 199.5p for diesel.

## Sewage Spills

Storm overflows within 4 miles of Cheltenham spilled [sewage](/cheltenham-sewage-overflows) 10 times in October, for 46.9 hours in total, up from one spill of 36 minutes in September.

## Reports on FixMyStreet

People reported [1,025 problems](/cheltenham-fix-my-street) on FixMyStreet in October, down from 1,441 in September.

## FAQs

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- Food hygiene ratings come from the Food Standards Agency, planning applications from Cheltenham Borough Council's planning register, street works from the Department for Transport's Street Manager, power cuts from National Grid Electricity Distribution, fuel prices from the GOV.UK fuel price scheme and the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero, sewage spills from the Water UK National Storm Overflow Hub and problem reports from FixMyStreet.

### Why Are Some Ratings From September?

- A rating is published after the business has had 21 days to appeal, and councils upload ratings in batches, so a rating published in October can be from an inspection in September.

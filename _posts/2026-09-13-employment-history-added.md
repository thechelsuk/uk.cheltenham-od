Cheltenham Open Data has added a [full employment rate history page](/cheltenham-employment-history), a companion to the current figures on the [All About Cheltenham page](/about-cheltenham).

It's the year-by-year data behind that headline employment rate — economic activity, employment and unemployment rates for Cheltenham residents aged 16-64, back to **2004**, as a chart and a table, sourced from the same ONS Nomis service.

The series covers **22** years, from 2004 to 2025.

## FAQs

### How Far Back Does the Data Go?

- To **2004** — **22** years of history in total, as far back as this particular Nomis dataset covers for Cheltenham.

### Why Does the Rate Jump Around so Much Year to Year?

- These rates come from a sample survey rather than a full count. For a town the size of Cheltenham, year-to-year swings of several percentage points are normal statistical noise, not necessarily a real change in the local economy — the overall trend matters more than any single year.

### Why Is the Unemployment Rate Missing for Some Years?

- The ONS suppresses the unemployment rate for a given year when the underlying sample is too small to be statistically reliable at local authority level. This happens intermittently throughout the whole series and isn't specific to Cheltenham.

### How Does This Relate to the All About Cheltenham Page?

- That page shows the current headline rates; this one shows how they got there, with the full year-by-year history and a chart.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- Nomis, the ONS's official service for labour market data, refreshed monthly.

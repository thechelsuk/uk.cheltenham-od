Cheltenham Open Data has added two new companions to the [Post Office locations page](/cheltenham-post-office-locations): an [InPost locker locations page](/cheltenham-inpost-lockers) and a [DPD Pickup points page](/cheltenham-dpd-pickup-points).

Between them they cover the parcel drop-off and collection options a Post Office branch list alone misses — self-service lockers open outside shop hours, and the wider network of local shops, newsagents and petrol station kiosks signed up to accept DPD parcels, useful if you're regularly sending items sold on Vinted, eBay or another marketplace.

The InPost page tracks **25** lockers around town, many open 24/7, and the DPD page tracks **32** pickup points, a mix of shops and Post Office branches.

## FAQs

### What's the Difference Between These Pages and the Post Office Page?

- Post Office branches are staffed counters with set opening hours. InPost lockers are unstaffed, self-service, and often open 24 hours a day. DPD Pickup points are local shops — convenience stores, newsagents, petrol stations — that have signed up to accept DPD drop-offs and collections alongside their normal business, frequently with longer hours than a Post Office branch.

### How Many InPost Lockers Are There in Cheltenham?

- **25**, sourced directly from InPost's own public locker location service.

### How Many DPD Pickup Points Are There?

- **32**, including several Post Office branches as well as independent shops like Premier, Family Shopper and Yeep! convenience stores, sourced from DPD's own pickup point finder.

### How Often Are These Pages Updated?

- Hourly, pulling straight from each courier's own public locator service — no manual list to go stale.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- InPost's own locations API and DPD's own pickup point finder API — the same services powering each courier's "find a locker/pickup point" tool on their own site.

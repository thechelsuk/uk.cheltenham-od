Gloucestershire will play the 2027 T20 Blast as Bristol Lions, and the fixtures are now out. Our [Gloucestershire cricket page](/cheltenham-sports/gloucestershire-cricket#t20-blast-fixtures) lists all 20 of the men's and women's Blast matches. The men's season opens at home to Durham on Friday 21 May, and the women start away at Kent two days later.

## What's on the Page

- **Men's fixtures:** 12 matches in Group B, six at home in Bristol, against Glamorgan, Northamptonshire Steelbacks, Somerset, Warwickshire Bears and Worcestershire Rapids, plus Durham and Kent Spitfires.
- **Women's fixtures:** eight matches in League Two, four at home.
- **Finals:** the quarter-finals are on 14 July, and men's Finals Day is on Saturday 17 July 2027 at Edgbaston. The women's League Two Finals Day is the same day in Bristol.

None of the 2027 Blast matches is at Cheltenham. Every home game is at the Seat Unique Stadium in Bristol. The dates of next summer's Cheltenham Cricket Festival haven't been announced yet, and we'll add them when they are.

## FAQs

### Why Are Gloucestershire Called Bristol Lions?

- From the 2027 season, Gloucestershire's T20 Blast sides play under the name Bristol Lions. The new name was announced on 24 November 2026.

### When Do Bristol Lions Play Their First Match?

- The men play Durham at the Seat Unique Stadium on Friday 21 May 2027. The women play Kent at Beckenham on Sunday 23 May 2027. Start times haven't been announced yet.

### Where Do These Fixtures Come From?

- From the England and Wales Cricket Board's published 2027 fixture lists. Fixtures can change, so check [Gloucestershire Cricket](https://www.gloscricket.co.uk/fixtures/) before you travel.

Cheltenham Open Data has published two new pages: [Cheltenham renewable energy](/cheltenham-renewable-energy) and [Cheltenham gas and electricity use](/cheltenham-renewable-energy/energy-use).

The renewable energy page counts the town's renewable installations, almost all of them solar panels, and shows how much they can generate and how that has grown each year since 2014. A map shows every larger solar, wind and battery storage project within 12 miles of the town centre, whether it is running, approved or still in planning.

The gas and electricity use page shows how much energy Cheltenham's homes and businesses use, how a typical home compares with the South West and England since 2005, and a map of use by ward and neighbourhood.

## FAQs

### What Does the Renewable Energy Page Show?

- The number of renewable installations in Cheltenham, their capacity and how much electricity they generated, year by year, and how the town compares with the rest of Gloucestershire.
- A map and table of larger renewable and battery projects nearby, with their planning status.

### What Does the Gas and Electricity Use Page Show?

- Total gas and electricity use in Cheltenham, the average and median used per home, and a map and tables for every ward and small area.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- The Department for Energy Security and Net Zero's [Regional Renewable Statistics](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/regional-renewable-statistics), [Renewable Energy Planning Database](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/renewable-energy-planning-database-quarterly-extract) and subnational [electricity](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/sub-national-electricity-consumption-data) and [gas](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/sub-national-gas-consumption-data) consumption statistics.

### How Often Are the Pages Updated?

- We check for new figures every month. Renewable installations are published each September, the planning database every three months, and gas and electricity use each December.

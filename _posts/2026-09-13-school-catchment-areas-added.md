Cheltenham Open Data has added two new maps to the [schools page](/cheltenham-schools): a [secondary school catchment areas map](/cheltenham-schools/catchment-areas/secondary) and a [primary school catchment areas map](/cheltenham-schools/catchment-areas/primary), each shading the town by whichever school sits closest to it in a straight line.

Gloucestershire doesn't publish fixed catchment boundaries — most admissions are decided by measured distance once a school is oversubscribed, alongside siblings and faith criteria. So rather than reproduce a boundary that doesn't exist, both maps compute their own "nearest school" zones directly from official school locations, **7** secondary schools and **32** primary schools in and around the town.

## FAQs

### Is This an Official Catchment Map?

- No. Gloucestershire County Council doesn't draw fixed catchment boundaries for its schools, so there's nothing official to map. These are straight-line "nearest school" approximations, not a reproduction of any council boundary or admissions guarantee.

### Which Schools Are Included?

- Schools within Cheltenham and its immediate edge. Schools sharing a Cheltenham postcode district but sitting in a separate village or town — Winchcombe, Bourton-on-the-Water and Stow-on-the-Wold among them — are excluded, since including them would stretch zones across open countryside rather than reflect real choices within Cheltenham.

### Why Two Separate Maps?

- Primary and secondary schools sit at very different densities — 7 secondary schools across the town versus 32 primaries — so each gets its own map and its own realistic exclusion radius rather than being squeezed onto one.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- The Department for Education's Get Information about Schools (GIAS) register, the same source behind the [full schools list](/cheltenham-schools), refreshed monthly. Grid references are converted from the register's Ordnance Survey coordinates.

### How Do I Check Admissions for a Specific Address?

- Use [Gloucestershire County Council's Find a School tool](https://www.gloucestershire.gov.uk/education-and-learning/find-a-school), the official source for distance measurements and admissions criteria.

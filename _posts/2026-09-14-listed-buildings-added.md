Cheltenham Open Data has published a new [listed buildings page](/cheltenham-listed-buildings), covering every entry on Historic England's National Heritage List for the town.

The page maps **1,151** listed buildings, colour-coded by grade, alongside Cheltenham's **7** scheduled monuments and **2** registered parks & gardens including Pittville Park. A sortable, filterable table sits alongside the map, with a direct link to each entry's official Historic England listing.

## FAQs

### What Does the Listed Buildings Page Show?

- A map and table of every listed building in Cheltenham, filterable by grade (I, II* or II), plus separate map overlays and a table for scheduled monuments and registered parks & gardens.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- Historic England's [National Heritage List for England](https://www.api.gov.uk/he/national-heritage-list-for-england-nhle/), the official register of nationally protected historic buildings and sites, released under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

### What Do the Grades Mean?

- Grade I is exceptional national importance, Grade II* is particularly important, and Grade II is of special interest. The grade reflects architectural or historic significance, not the building's condition or public access.

### How Often Is It Updated?

- Monthly, since listings change infrequently. The "last updated" date is at the bottom of the page.

### Can I Visit These Buildings?

- Being listed doesn't grant public access — most entries are private property. Some, like churches and Pittville Park, are open to the public; check locally before visiting.

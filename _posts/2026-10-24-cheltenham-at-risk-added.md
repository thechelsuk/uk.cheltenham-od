Three new pages bring together what could go wrong in Cheltenham and what you can do about it.

[Cheltenham at risk](/cheltenham-at-risk) sets out the seven top risks in Gloucestershire's Community Risk Register: terrorism, infectious disease, electricity supply failure, flooding, fuel supply disruption, cyber attack and extreme weather. Each one sits on a matrix of likelihood against impact, with a short summary of the advice and links to the pages on this site where you can follow it live, from flood warnings and power cuts to the UK threat level.

A cyber attack is the only risk with the top likelihood score, so the new [cyber security](/cheltenham-cyber-security) page explains who to tell about fraud, scams and cyber crime, and shows how often UK businesses and charities are attacked: 43% of businesses reported a breach or attack in the government's latest survey.

The new [guide to staying safe online](/staying-safe-online) covers the steps that protect against most attacks, including password managers, two-step verification, passkeys, data breach alerts and the warning signs of a scam.

We've also added Action Fraud, the Anti-Terrorist Hotline and the 7726 scam text number to our [helplines and contacts](/cheltenham-helpful-contacts-reports).

## FAQs

### What Is the Community Risk Register?

- A public summary of the emergencies most likely to affect Gloucestershire, or that would do the most harm, published by the Gloucestershire Local Resilience Forum. It scores each risk from 1 to 5 for likelihood and impact.

### Which Risk Has the Highest Impact?

- Terrorism and infectious disease share the top impact score of 5. Terrorism is also rated more likely, with a likelihood score of 4.

### Where Can I Report a Scam?

- Report fraud and cyber crime to Action Fraud on 0300 123 2040 or online, forward scam texts to 7726 and scam emails to <report@phishing.gov.uk>. If you've lost money, call your bank first.

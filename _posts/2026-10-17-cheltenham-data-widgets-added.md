Cheltenham Open Data now offers free [data widgets](/widgets): small boxes of local figures that anyone can put on their own site by copying a short piece of code.

There are four to start with. **House prices** shows the median price of homes sold in Cheltenham over the last 12 months, with the number of sales and new builds. **Rents** shows the average private rent and what one to four bedroom homes cost. **Affordability** shows how many homes sold for 4.5 times typical full-time pay or less. **Cheapest fuel** shows the lowest unleaded and diesel prices within 20 miles, updated every hour.

Each widget updates itself whenever new figures are published, so the numbers on your page never go out of date. They work in WordPress, Squarespace, Wix and any other site that lets you add HTML, and they can match a light or dark design.

## FAQs

### Who Are the Widgets For?

- Anyone with a site who wants local figures on it, such as estate and letting agents, mortgage brokers, local news sites, community groups and bloggers.

### Do They Cost Anything?

- No. They are free for personal and commercial sites, with no sign-up, and they set no cookies.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- HM Land Registry, the Office for National Statistics and the GOV.UK fuel price scheme, all published under the Open Government Licence. Each widget names its source and links back to the full page.

### Can I Ask for Another Widget?

- Yes. [Get in touch](/contact) with the local data you would like to show.

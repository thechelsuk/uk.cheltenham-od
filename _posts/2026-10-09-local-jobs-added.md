The [Cheltenham classifieds](/cheltenham-classifieds#jobs) now include new job vacancies from in and around Cheltenham every day, alongside adverts from local businesses and the community.

Each listing gives the job title, employer, location, pay, hours and closing date, with a short summary of the role and a link to read the full advert and apply on GOV.UK.

## FAQs

### Where Do the Jobs Come From?

- From GOV.UK's Work Hub job search, the government's free job service. Vacancies posted within five miles of Cheltenham are checked several times a day and added to the Jobs section of the classifieds.

### How Long Does Each Job Stay Listed?

- A week from when it's added, or until the job's closing date if that comes sooner. Older vacancies drop off automatically, so the list stays current.

### How Do I Apply?

- Every listing links to the full advert on GOV.UK, where you can read the details and apply directly with the employer.

### Can I Advertise a Job Myself?

- Yes. Local employers can still [post a free classified advert](/submission) for a vacancy, which appears in the same Jobs section.

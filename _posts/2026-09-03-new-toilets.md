Cheltenham Open Data has added a [list of public toilets in Cheltenham](/cheltenham-public-toilets) to the site.

Each entry shows what you need to know when you get caught short: whether it is wheelchair accessible, has baby changing, is free to use or charges a small fee, and whether a RADAR key is required. Opening hours are listed where known, and every location links straight through to Google Maps.

The list covers town centre facilities, parks and gardens such as Montpellier Gardens, Pittville Park and Sandford Park, transport hubs including Cheltenham Spa Station, and a range of supermarkets, filling stations and shops that open their toilets to the public.

The data comes from the Toilet Map 2026 dataset, made available under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence. Because it is built from user submissions it may contain errors or omission, and the occasional joke listing, so do check locally if you are relying on a particular facility. Any changes to the dataset are reflected on the site as they are published.

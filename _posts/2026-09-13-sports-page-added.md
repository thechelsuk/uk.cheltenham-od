Cheltenham Open Data has added a [Sport in Cheltenham](/cheltenham-sports) page, covering the town's professional and semi-professional clubs alongside grassroots running, hiking and climbing groups.

Two areas get their own data-backed sub-page: [Cheltenham parkrun](/cheltenham-sports/parkrun) maps all **four** local parkrun events, and [Cheltenham Town FC](/cheltenham-sports/cheltenham-town-fc) shows the next fixture, most recent result, and how to get to Whaddon Road by train, car or Park & Ride.

## FAQs

### What's on the Main Sports Page?

- Cheltenham Town FC, Gloucester Rugby and Cheltenham Town Women, plus a curated list of local running, hiking and climbing clubs — including parkrun, Cheltenham & County Harriers, CLC Striders, Almost Athletes, Cheltenham Rambling Club and Boulders Cheltenham.

### Why Is This Page Curated Rather than Sourced from Open Data?

- Unlike most of this site, there's no open register of local sports clubs to pull from — governing bodies like England Athletics and British Cycling only offer web search tools, not data feeds. So the main page is a hand-checked list rather than an automated one; every link was verified individually before publishing.

### Where Does the Parkrun Data Come From?

- Parkrun's own public events feed, refreshed monthly since event locations rarely change.

### Where Does the Cheltenham Town FC Data Come From?

- TheSportsDB's free API, refreshed daily. Its free tier only returns the single most recent result and next fixture — for the full season, the page links to the club's own results and fixtures page.

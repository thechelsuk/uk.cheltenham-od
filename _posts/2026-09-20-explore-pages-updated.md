Cheltenham Open Data has refreshed the six Explore category pages so that each one describes everything in its section.

As the site has grown, several pages had gone missing from the descriptions. Each category page now covers its full set of pages:

- [Getting Around](/explore/getting-around) — fuel prices are now described as checked hourly, and broadband has moved to where it belongs under Home & Property. Cycle routes now mention the local pump tracks and bike parks.
- [Home & Property](/explore/home-property) — now covers [housing supply](/cheltenham-house-prices/housing-supply), the [total number of properties](/cheltenham-house-prices/council-tax-stock) over time, and [mobile coverage](/cheltenham-mobile-coverage) alongside broadband.
- [Safety & Environment](/explore/safety-environment) — now includes [power cuts](/cheltenham-power-cuts) and links to the flood warnings, power cuts and Fix My Street history pages.
- [Things to Do](/explore/things-to-do) — sport now mentions [Cheltenham Town FC](/cheltenham-sports/cheltenham-town-fc) and [parkrun](/cheltenham-sports/parkrun) by name, and [third spaces](/cheltenham-third-spaces) is pointed to under Community & Support, where it lives.
- [Community & Support](/explore/community-support) — the summary now includes third spaces.
- [About & Info](/explore/about-info) — now covers [politics](/cheltenham-politics), the [party history](/cheltenham-politics/party-history) and the [council land and assets](/cheltenham-council-land-and-assets) map.

The council tax description has also been corrected: charges are shown by band for the borough and the five parishes that set their own precept, rather than every parish.

## FAQs

### What Are the Explore Pages?

- Six category pages that group the site's data by theme, so you can browse everything on a subject in one place rather than searching for individual pages.

### Where Do I Find Them?

- Each category is linked from the [home page](/) under Explore by Category.

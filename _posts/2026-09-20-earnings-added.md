Cheltenham Open Data has published a new [earnings history page](/cheltenham-earnings-history), showing what the typical full-time employee earns in Cheltenham and how that has changed since 2008.

It charts median annual pay for people who live in Cheltenham and for people who work in the town, alongside Gloucestershire, the South West and England. A table compares the latest year's annual, weekly and hourly medians, a second chart shows men's and women's median pay, and a year-by-year table lists every figure with the ONS's own rating of how reliable each estimate is.

The page sits alongside [employment rates](/cheltenham-employment-history) under [All About Cheltenham](/about-cheltenham).

## FAQs

### What Does the Earnings Page Show?

- Median full-time pay in Cheltenham for every year since 2008, by where people live and where they work, compared with Gloucestershire, the South West and England, plus a men and women split.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- The Office for National Statistics' Annual Survey of Hours and Earnings, accessed through [Nomis](https://www.nomisweb.co.uk/) and published under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

### How Often Is It Updated?

- The survey is published once a year in the autumn. The page is refreshed monthly, so the new year appears soon after release.

### Why Does It Start in 2008?

- The ONS earnings figures available for Cheltenham start in 2008.

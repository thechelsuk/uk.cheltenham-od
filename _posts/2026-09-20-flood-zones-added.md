Cheltenham Open Data has published a new [flood zones map](/cheltenham-flood-zones), showing the land around Cheltenham that the Environment Agency puts in Flood Zone 2 and Flood Zone 3.

Flood Zone 3, in darker blue, is land with a 1 in 100 or greater chance of river flooding in any year, and Flood Zone 2, in lighter blue, has a 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000 chance. The page explains what the zones mean, why they ignore flood defences and leave out surface water flooding, which caused much of the damage in [2007](/cheltenham-flood-warnings/2007-floods), and where to check the flood risk for a specific address.

It sits alongside the [flood warnings and live river levels](/cheltenham-flood-warnings).

## FAQs

### What Does the Flood Zones Page Show?

- A map of Flood Zone 2 and Flood Zone 3 land around Cheltenham, with an explanation of what the zones mean.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- The Environment Agency's [Flood Map for Planning](https://environment.data.gov.uk/dataset/04532375-a198-476e-985e-0579a0a11b47), published under the Open Government Licence. It is updated by the Environment Agency as needed, and the page is refreshed when it changes.

### Can I Use It to Check My Home?

- Only as a guide. The outlines are simplified, so use the Environment Agency's long term flood risk service for a specific address.

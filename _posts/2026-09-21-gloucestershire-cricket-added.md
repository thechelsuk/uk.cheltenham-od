Cheltenham Open Data has published a new [Gloucestershire cricket page](/cheltenham-sports/gloucestershire-cricket), covering the county's men's and women's teams and the Cheltenham Cricket Festival.

Most of Gloucestershire's home matches are in Bristol, but every summer the club brings cricket to the College Ground at Cheltenham College, the home of the longest-running cricket festival on an outfield in the world. The page has a map of both home grounds, a table with their addresses and how to get there, and a Google Maps directions link for each. Fixtures, results and tickets change all the time, so the page links to the club's official pages for them rather than repeating them.

It sits under [sport & clubs](/cheltenham-sports) next to the Cheltenham Town FC and parkrun pages, and the [festivals](/festivals) page now mentions the cricket festival.

## FAQs

### What Does the Cricket Page Show?

- The men's and women's teams, the Cheltenham Cricket Festival, a map and directions to the Bristol and Cheltenham grounds, and links to official fixtures, results and tickets.

### Where Do the Details Come From?

- Gloucestershire Cricket and Cheltenham College. The page is curated by hand, so always check the club's own site for current fixtures and travel information.

### When Is the Cheltenham Cricket Festival?

- It is held each summer. The 2026 festival ran from 8 to 23 August, and the club announces the dates for each year's festival.

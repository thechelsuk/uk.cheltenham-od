Cheltenham Open Data has added a new [power cuts page](/cheltenham-power-cuts), alongside our existing [flood warnings](/cheltenham-flood-warnings) and [bus disruptions](/cheltenham-bus-data) tracking.

It pulls the live feed from National Grid Electricity Distribution — the electricity network operator for Gloucestershire, formerly Western Power Distribution — showing any current power cut affecting a Cheltenham postcode (GL50–GL54) on a map, plus a rolling history of past incidents and an [Atom feed](/feeds/power-cuts.xml) to subscribe to. A live power cut also now surfaces as an alert on the homepage.

## FAQs

### What Does the Power Cuts Page Show?

- A map and table of any power cut currently affecting a Cheltenham postcode, a history of past incidents recorded since this page went live, and links to report or check a power cut by phone.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- National Grid Electricity Distribution's [Live Power Cuts open dataset](https://connecteddata.nationalgrid.co.uk/dataset/live-power-cuts).

### What Should I Do in a Power Cut?

- Call 105, the free 24/7 national power cut number, to report it or check for known outages in your area. Call 999 too if there's a risk to life, such as damage to power lines or a substation.

### Why Is the List Usually Empty?

- Most of the time there's no power cut affecting Cheltenham, so an empty list and map are expected — the same as the flood warnings page most of the year.

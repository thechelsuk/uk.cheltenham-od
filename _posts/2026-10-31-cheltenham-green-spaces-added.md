Our new [parks and green spaces map](/cheltenham-green-spaces) draws every public park, garden, playing field, allotment, cemetery and churchyard in Cheltenham to its real outline.

Ordnance Survey records 214 of them in the borough, covering 366 hectares, or 7.9% of its land. Pittville is the greenest ward: Pittville Park, the Approach Golf Course and Midwinter Allotments help make green space 29.8% of the ward. Each site is listed with its size, its ward and how many ways in it has.

Every [ward page](/cheltenham-wards) now shows how much of the ward is green space, and its list of nearby parks and open spaces has been rebuilt from the same map, with names and sizes in place of council property addresses.

## FAQs

### What Does the Green Spaces Page Show?

- A map of every site, shaded by type: parks and gardens, sports grounds and playing fields, play spaces, allotments, and cemeteries and churchyards. Tables compare all 20 wards and list every site, largest first, with a filter by type.

### Is There Anything for My Ward?

- Yes. The green spaces page compares every ward's share of green space, and each ward page has a new card with its own figure and an updated list of the parks in and around it.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- [OS Open Greenspace](https://www.ordnancesurvey.co.uk/products/os-open-greenspace) from Ordnance Survey, published under the Open Government Licence.

### How Often Is It Updated?

- Ordnance Survey updates the dataset twice a year, and the page checks for a new release every month.

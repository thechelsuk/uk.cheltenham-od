The [planning applications page](/cheltenham-planning-applications) now has a map, so you can see what is being planned near you as well as reading the list.

Each application from the last 90 days, including those that already have a decision, appears as a pin. Select a pin to see the reference, address, a short description, the date received and the current status, with a link to the council's full case file. Both tables also have a pin link for each application that opens its location in Google Maps.

Pins sit at the centre of each application's postcode, so they show the area rather than the exact site. Applications with no postcode in their address can't be placed, and applications in the same postcode share a pin.

## FAQs

### What Does the Map Show?

- Recent planning applications submitted to Cheltenham Borough Council, and those decided in the last 90 days, placed by postcode.

### How Accurate Are the Pins?

- They mark the centre of the postcode, which is close to the site but not exact. Always check the council's record for the precise location.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- The applications come from Cheltenham Borough Council's planning portal, refreshed daily, and the locations from postcodes.io using Office for National Statistics postcode data.

### Why Is an Application Missing From the Map?

- Some applications have no postcode in their address in the council's register, so they appear in the tables but can't be placed on the map.

One in eight of Cheltenham's business properties stood empty in October 2026. Our new [Cheltenham business rates](/cheltenham-business-rates) page shows the borough council's latest lists of occupied and empty business properties, with rateable values by band and type on a map. A second page lists every [empty business property](/cheltenham-business-rates/empty-properties), with its exemption from empty rates and how many of the council's lists have shown it empty.

## What the Pages Show

- **Occupied and empty:** 498 of the council's 3,992 business properties were empty in its October 2026 list, 12.5% of the total.
- **Long-term empty:** 364 of those were empty in every one of the council's four lists since January 2026.
- **Offices more often empty:** 18.9% of offices were empty, against 9.2% of shops.
- **Paying no empty rates:** 152 empty properties are listed buildings, which pay no rates while they stay empty.
- **A map by rateable value:** every postcode with a business property, shaded by rateable value, with a filter for each band and for empty properties.

The pages check for a new list from the council every week.

## FAQs

### Does the List Name the Owners?

- No. The council leaves out the names of sole traders and individuals, and our pages leave out every ratepayer's name. Holiday lets are counted but not listed or mapped, as many are in or next to someone's home.

### What Is a Rateable Value?

- The Valuation Office Agency's estimate of the yearly rent a property could be let for on the open market, set at 1 April 2024 for the values in force from April 2026. A property's business rates bill starts from its rateable value.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- From [Cheltenham Borough Council's business rates data](https://www.cheltenham.gov.uk/business-and-licensing/business-rates-and-rate-relief/business-rates-data/), which it publishes a few times a year.

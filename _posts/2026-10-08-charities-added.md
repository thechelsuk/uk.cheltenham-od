Cheltenham Open Data has published a new [charities page](/cheltenham-charities), listing every registered charity based in Cheltenham and the GL50–GL54 postcodes.

There are 587 of them, from village hall committees and school parent associations to national organisations headquartered in the town, with a combined income of more than £400 million in their latest accounts. You can filter the list by cause or search it by name, then follow each charity through to its entry on the Charity Commission's register.

## FAQs

### What Does the Charities Page Show?

- Every main registered charity with a Cheltenham contact postcode, with what it does in its own words, its latest income, the year it registered and a link to its own site where it has one.
- How many charities work on each cause, from education and poverty relief to amateur sport and animals, and how many fall into each income band.

### Why Isn't There a Map?

- Many small charities give a trustee's home address as their contact details, so the page doesn't publish addresses, phone numbers or emails. Each charity's entry on the register has the contact details it has chosen to make public.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- The Charity Commission for England and Wales's register of charities, published under the Open Government Licence. The page is refreshed monthly.

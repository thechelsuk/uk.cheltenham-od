Cheltenham Open Data has added a [crime data by neighbourhoods in cheltenham](/cheltenham-crime-data) to the site.

Pulling data from the [UK Police API](https://data.police.uk/docs/), the site now shows monthly neighbourhood-level crime figures for Cheltenham, broken down by month and neighbourhood.

The data available goes back to August 2023.

## FAQs

### What’s the Safest Neighbourhood in Cheltenham?

- According to the data, the area with the lowest reported street crime data is [Lansdown with a reported **342** street crimes in 2025](/cheltenham-crime-data/2025). Followed closely by Prestbury (**368**), Springbank and Fiddlers Green (**372**).
- [According to 2024 data Lansdown had 283 reported street crimes](/cheltenham-crime-data/2024), followed by Fairview (289) and Leckhampton (**352**)

### What’s the Worst Neighbourhood in Cheltenham?

- According to the data, The town centre area had a total of **3,356** street crimes in 2024, and 3268 in 2025.

### How Many Reported Crimes Were There Across Cheltenham?

- The totals below are also calculated from the monthly neighbourhood records, rather than copied from a separate summary:

  - 2024: **12,698** reported crimes.
  - 2025: **12,745** reported crimes.
  - 2026: **1,030** reported crimes.

### Did Reported Crime Rise or Fall In 2025?

- There were **47** more reported crimes across Cheltenham in 2025 than in 2024, an increase from **12,698** to **12,745**.

### Which Month Had the Most Reported Crime?

- March 2024 was the busiest complete-year month in the dataset, with **1,216** reported crimes.

### Which Neighbourhood Saw the Largest Increase In 2025?

- Swindon Village and Wyman's Brook increased from **854** reported crimes in 2024 to **1011** in 2025, an increase of **157**.

### What Does a Reported Crime Count Mean?

- These are incidents described as 'street-crime' returned by the [UK Police API](https://data.police.uk/docs/method/crime-street/) for the selected neighbourhood and month. They are reports recorded by the police, not a measure of how safe or unsafe every resident or street is. The figures can change when the source data is updated.

### Which Months Are Included?

- The dataset currently covers monthly figures from August 2023 through July 2026. A year total in this post means January to December for that calendar year; the 2023 and 2026 data is currently partial. Figures will be updated when new data is available.

### Why Is 2026 Data so Low?

- There appears to be a change or broken process in the way the data is reported to data team at the Police, as there has been approximately 1,000 crimes per month from Aug 2024 to January 2026, but since then, only 20 crimes have been documented over the next 6 months to July 2026. This has been reported to the Police Data Team. Data will be updated when new records are provided.

### Why Is There Not More Recent Data?

Police data is typically updated about one month after the month has completed, e.g. January's data is published in March and so on. There is a privacy element to this as well as a process for anonymising the data.

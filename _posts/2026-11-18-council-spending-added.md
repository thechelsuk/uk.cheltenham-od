Cheltenham Open Data has published a new [council spending page](/cheltenham-council-spending), which shows every payment of £500 or more that Cheltenham Borough Council makes to its suppliers. In the 12 months to September 2026, the council paid out £71,129,966, and its biggest supplier was Ubico Limited, paid £12,330,788.

The page adds up the council's monthly files by month and by financial year back to 2016-17. It comes with a page for each of the last five years, the council's [100 biggest suppliers](/cheltenham-council-spending/suppliers) and its [net spending by service](/cheltenham-council-spending/accounts).

## FAQs

### What Does the Council Spending Page Show?

- Monthly and yearly totals of what the council paid its suppliers, the biggest suppliers and service areas over the last 12 months, and every payment in the latest month, which you can filter by service area.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- From the payments to suppliers files that [Cheltenham Borough Council](https://www.cheltenham.gov.uk/about-the-council/council-performance-and-spending/financial-information/payments-to-suppliers-and-procurement-card-purchases/) publishes each month. The accounts page uses the government's [revenue outturn figures](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/local-authority-revenue-expenditure-and-financing) for each council.

### How Often Is It Updated?

- The page checks for a new monthly file every week. The council publishes each month's payments some weeks after the month ends.

### Can I See Spending in My Ward?

- No. The council's files don't record where in Cheltenham money was spent, so spending can't be split by ward.

Cheltenham Open Data has published a new [deprivation page](/cheltenham-deprivation), mapping the English Indices of Deprivation 2025 for all 77 of Cheltenham's neighbourhoods.

Overall, Cheltenham is one of the less deprived places in England, ranking 234th of 296 districts, where 1 is the most deprived. But the map shows how much that varies across the town, with a handful of neighbourhoods in the most deprived fifth of England a short walk from some of the least deprived anywhere. Every [ward page](/cheltenham-wards) now shows how its own neighbourhoods rank, too.

## FAQs

### What Does the Deprivation Page Show?

- A map of every neighbourhood, shaded from most to least deprived, which you can switch between overall deprivation and each kind: income, employment, education, health, crime, housing and living environment.
- A table ranking Cheltenham's 20 wards, and a table of every neighbourhood with its national rank and deciles.

### What Are the Indices of Deprivation?

- The government's official measure of relative deprivation, ranking all 33,755 small neighbourhoods in England. They're published every four to six years; the 2025 edition replaces the 2019 one.

### What Has Changed on the Ward Pages?

- Each ward page now shows where the ward ranks among Cheltenham's 20 wards, and a table of its neighbourhoods with their scores for each kind of deprivation.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- The Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government's English Indices of Deprivation 2025, published under the Open Government Licence, matched to neighbourhood and ward boundaries from the Office for National Statistics.

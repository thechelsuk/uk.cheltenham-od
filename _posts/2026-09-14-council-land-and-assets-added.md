Cheltenham Open Data has published a new [council land & assets page](/cheltenham-council-land-and-assets).

The page maps and tabulates every property and piece of land Cheltenham Borough Council reported owning or holding, as required under the Local Government Transparency Code — car parks, parks, offices, housing land and more, each with its tenure type and how the council holds it. A [2021 snapshot](/cheltenham-council-land-and-assets/2021) of the same dataset is also available alongside the current 2023 one.

## FAQs

### What Does the Land & Assets Page Show?

- A map and sortable table of every asset in Cheltenham Borough Council's land and assets return, including its address, tenure type (freehold or leasehold), tenure detail and holding type.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- Cheltenham Borough Council originally published this under the [Local Government Transparency Code](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/local-government-transparency-code-2015), but the council's own open data page for it has since gone offline. This page uses a public [GitHub mirror](https://github.com/chrismytton/cheltenham-council-land-and-assets) of the original CSV exports, kept by developer Chris Mytton — who also built [his own map of the data](https://www.chrismytton.com/cheltenham-council-land-and-assets/).

### Why Two Different Years?

- The council published a return in 2021 and again in 2023, using a slightly different set of columns each time. Rather than pick one, both snapshots are on the site so you can compare.

### Is This Every Council Asset?

- It's everything in the council's own transparency return for that year — it won't necessarily include every asset acquired or disposed of since, and since the source page is offline there's no newer official return to check it against.

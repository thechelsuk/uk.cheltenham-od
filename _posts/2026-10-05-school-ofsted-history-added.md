Cheltenham Open Data now has an Ofsted page for 63 schools across GL50–GL54, linked from the [schools in Cheltenham](/cheltenham-schools) list.

Each page brings a school's whole Ofsted record together: its latest report card or graded inspection, area by area, and every report Ofsted has published for it, with a link to each one. The schools list now shows each school's latest Ofsted outcome too, so you can compare them at a glance.

## FAQs

### What Does Each School Page Show?

- The school's latest Ofsted outcome, how many reports are on record and since when, and its safeguarding judgement where it has a report card.
- The grades for each area from its latest report card or graded inspection.
- A table of every inspection, newest first, with the type of inspection, its outcome and a link to the full report.

### Which Schools Are Included?

- Every state school, special school and college in GL50–GL54 that Ofsted inspects, with new schools added as Ofsted inspects them.
- Independent schools such as Cheltenham College, Cheltenham Ladies' College and Dean Close are inspected by the Independent Schools Inspectorate rather than Ofsted, so they don't have a page.

### What Are Ofsted Report Cards?

- Since November 2025, Ofsted has graded each area of a school's work, such as inclusion, achievement, and attendance and behaviour, on a five-point scale from exceptional to urgent improvement, with safeguarding judged as met or not met. Older inspections gave a single overall grade: outstanding, good, requires improvement or inadequate.

### Why Do Some Schools Show Reports Under a Different Name or Number?

- When a school becomes an academy or merges with another, it gets a new reference number, and Ofsted files its earlier reports under the old one. The pages include those earlier reports so you can see the full history in one place.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- From [Ofsted](https://reports.ofsted.gov.uk/)'s inspection reports and its monthly management information on school inspections, with links between old and new schools from the Department for Education's Get Information about Schools register.

### How Often Is It Updated?

- Once a month, so a new inspection report appears on the school's page within a few weeks of Ofsted publishing it.

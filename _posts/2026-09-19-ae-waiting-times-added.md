Cheltenham Open Data has published a new [A&E waiting times page](/cheltenham-ae-waiting-times), showing monthly A&E figures for Gloucestershire Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust, which runs Cheltenham General Hospital and Gloucestershire Royal Hospital.

The page leads with the latest month: how many people attended A&E, the share seen within four hours, and how many patients waited 12 hours or more between a decision to admit them and being admitted. Below that, two charts and a sortable table show the same figures month by month for the last three years.

It also maps the trust's hospitals within 10 miles of Cheltenham, with their addresses and whether the NHS directory lists an A&E at each site.

This joins [GPs and pharmacies](/cheltenham-gp-pharmacies) and [food banks](/cheltenham-foodbank-needed-items) under Community & Support.

## FAQs

### What Does the A&E Waiting Times Page Show?

- The latest monthly A&E figures, 36 months of history in charts and a table, and a map and table of the trust's hospitals near Cheltenham.

### Do the Figures Cover Cheltenham General Hospital on Its Own?

- No. NHS England publishes the figures for the whole trust, so they combine Cheltenham General Hospital and Gloucestershire Royal Hospital and can't be split by site.

### Does the Page Show How Busy A&E Is Right Now?

- No. The figures cover a whole month and are published after it has ended. In an emergency, call 999, and for urgent help that isn't an emergency use [NHS 111](https://111.nhs.uk/).

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- The A&E figures come from [NHS England's A&E statistics](https://www.england.nhs.uk/statistics/statistical-work-areas/ae-waiting-times-and-activity/), and the hospitals from the [NHS Organisation Data Service](https://digital.nhs.uk/services/organisation-data-service). Both are published under the Open Government Licence.

### How Often Is It Updated?

- NHS England publishes new figures on the second Thursday of each month, covering the previous month. The page checks for them every day, and the hospitals list is refreshed monthly.

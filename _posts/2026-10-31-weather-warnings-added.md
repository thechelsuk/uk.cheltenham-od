Our [Cheltenham 10-day weather forecast](/cheltenham-10-day-weather-forecast) now opens with any Met Office weather warnings in force or coming up, checked every five minutes.

When a warning is live it also appears in the alerts on our homepage, and each one links to the Met Office, where its map shows exactly which areas it covers. Every warning is logged on the new [weather warnings history](/cheltenham-10-day-weather-forecast/weather-warnings) page, and you can follow new ones through the [weather warnings feed](/feeds/weather-warnings.xml) or our [all-in-one alerts feed](/feeds/alerts.xml), alongside flood warnings, power cuts and road closures.

## FAQs

### Which Warnings Are Shown?

- Every Met Office warning that lists Gloucestershire among the areas it covers. A warning for Gloucestershire may cover only part of the county, such as the Forest of Dean or the Cotswolds, so open it on the Met Office site to see whether Cheltenham is inside the area.

### What Do Yellow, Amber and Red Warnings Mean?

- Yellow means be aware, amber means be prepared for disruption, and red means dangerous weather is expected and you should act to keep yourself and others safe.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- The [Met Office's weather warnings](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/weather/warnings-and-advice/uk-warnings), shown in its own words.

### How Often Is It Updated?

- The warnings are checked every five minutes. The forecast itself still updates every day.

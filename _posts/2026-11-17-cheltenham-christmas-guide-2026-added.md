Cheltenham's Christmas lights go on across the town on Saturday 21 November, and our new [guide to Christmas in Cheltenham 2026](/cheltenham-christmas-guide-2026) brings the season's dates together in one place. Every event links to its organiser or an official source, so you can check times and book before you go. Alongside the switch-ons, markets and Santa, it covers the practical side of winter: school holidays, buses and how the roads are gritted.

## What's in the Guide

- **Lights and switch-ons:** the town-wide switch-on on 21 November, the Pittville Pump Room switch-on on 28 November, and the light show on the Municipal Offices from 3 to 16 December.
- **Markets and late-night shopping:** the Christmas market on the Promenade from 10 to 23 December, with late-night shopping and free council parking on two Thursday evenings.
- **Carols and concerts:** Advent carols at the Minster, the Mayor's Christmas Cracker, and Handel's Messiah at the Town Hall.
- **Meeting Santa:** grottos, a steam train, afternoon teas and a theatre show, with prices where they've been published.
- **School and bank holidays:** when schools break up and go back, and the Christmas bank holidays.
- **Buses, roads and gritting:** what's known so far about Christmas buses and road closures, and how Gloucestershire County Council decides which roads to grit.

We'll update the guide as new details are announced, including Stagecoach's Christmas and New Year timetables.

## FAQs

### When Do Gloucestershire Schools Break Up for Christmas?

- Schools whose term dates are set by Gloucestershire County Council break up on Friday 18 December 2026 and go back on Monday 4 January 2027. Academies and some other schools set their own dates, so check with your child's school.

### Where Does This Information Come From?

- From each event's organiser and from official sources, including Gloucestershire County Council, The Cheltenham Trust, Cheltenham BID, Stagecoach West and the Jockey Club. The [guide](/cheltenham-christmas-guide-2026) links to each one.

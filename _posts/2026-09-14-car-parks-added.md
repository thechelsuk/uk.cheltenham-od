Cheltenham Open Data has published a new [car parks page](/cheltenham-car-parks), covering public car parks in and around the town.

The page maps **26** car parks, from Cheltenham Borough Council's town centre pay car parks to the Arle Court Park & Ride and a scattering of free car parks further out, sourced from OpenStreetMap. A sortable table sits alongside the map showing operator, capacity, fee, charging hours and max stay where OpenStreetMap has that recorded.

## FAQs

### What Does the Car Parks Page Show?

- A map and sortable table of public car parks near Cheltenham, with operator, number of spaces, whether a fee applies, charging hours and max stay where recorded.

### Where Does the Data Come From?

- OpenStreetMap, via the Overpass API, filtered to car parks that are genuinely open to the public rather than private, customer-only or residents' parking. Released under the Open Database Licence (ODbL).

### Why Do Some Car Parks Have Missing Details?

- OpenStreetMap's data is only as complete as what's been mapped for each car park — some have full details, others just a name and location. Always check the signage on arrival, especially for max stay.

### How Often Is It Updated?

- Monthly, since car park locations, fees and hours rarely change. The "last updated" date is at the bottom of the page.

Fix My Street FAQs

Who Fixes Problems Reported Here?

It depends on the problem. Gloucestershire County Council looks after roads, pavements, street lights, road signs, drains and public footpaths. Cheltenham Borough Council handles litter, fly-tipping, graffiti and parks. FixMyStreet sends each report to the right authority for you.

How Do I Report a Problem?

Visit FixMyStreet, enter a postcode or street name, drop a pin on the map, and add a short description and a photo if you can.

How Do I Check the Status of a Report?

Click any report in the list to open it on FixMyStreet, where you can see whether it is open, investigating, fixed or closed, and read any update from the council.

Why Do Some Reports Appear Outside Cheltenham?

The list covers reports within 10km of the town centre, which includes Bishop's Cleeve, Churchdown and parts of the neighbouring Tewkesbury and Cotswold districts.

Can I See Older Reports?

Yes — the report history page keeps the last {{ site.data["fix-my-street-history"].retention_days }} days of reports.

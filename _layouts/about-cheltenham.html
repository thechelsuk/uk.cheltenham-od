Frequently Asked Questions

Where Does This Data Come From?

  • Population and employment figures come from Nomis, the Office for National Statistics' official service for labour market and census data. Population estimates and Census 2021 figures are both a count of usual residents; the small difference between them is expected, since they use slightly different methods and reference dates.

Why Isn't the Unemployment Rate Always Shown?

  • Local unemployment rates are modelled from a survey sample, and for smaller areas like a single local authority, the ONS sometimes suppresses the figure as too statistically unreliable to publish. This is normal and not specific to Cheltenham.

How Often Is This Updated?

  • Population estimates and employment rates are refreshed by the ONS periodically throughout the year; this page re-fetches the latest published figures from Nomis monthly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Charities Are Included?

  • Every charity on the Charity Commission's register for England and Wales whose contact address is in the Cheltenham postcodes GL50 to GL54, which includes the villages and small towns around Cheltenham.
  • Charities based elsewhere that work in Cheltenham, and small charities that don't have to register, such as those with an income under £5,000, aren't listed.

Why Isn't There a Map or Contact Details?

  • Many small charities give a trustee's home address, phone number or email as their contact details, so we don't republish them. Each charity's entry on the register, linked in the Register column, has the contact details it has chosen to make public.

What Does the Income Figure Show?

  • The total income in the charity's most recent accounts filed with the Charity Commission, which can be up to a year or two old. It includes donations, grants, fees and trading income, so a large figure doesn't always mean a charity relies on donations.

How Can I Check a Charity Is Genuine?

  • Look it up on the Charity Commission's register of charities using its name or registered charity number, and check its name and number match any appeal or collection. A registered charity's entry shows its latest accounts and when they were filed.

Where Does This Data Come From?

  • The Charity Commission's full register download, which it updates daily, published under the Open Government Licence. This page is refreshed monthly.

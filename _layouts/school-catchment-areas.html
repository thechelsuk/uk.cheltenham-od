FAQs

Why Isn't This an Official Catchment Map?

Because Gloucestershire doesn't have one. Unlike some local authorities, Gloucestershire County Council doesn't draw fixed catchment boundaries for {{ page.phase | downcase }} schools — it ranks applicants by measured distance once a school is oversubscribed, alongside criteria such as siblings and faith. The zones on this map are a straight-line "nearest school" approximation calculated from official school locations, not a reproduction of any council boundary.

Which Schools Are Included?

Cheltenham's {{ page.phase | downcase }} schools that fall within the town and its immediate edge. Schools sharing a Cheltenham postcode district but sitting in a separate village or town further out — {% if page.phase == "Primary" %}places like Winchcombe, Bourton-on-the-Water and Stow-on-the-Wold{% else %}Winchcombe School and The Cotswold Academy in Fairford{% endif %} — are excluded, since including them would stretch zones across open countryside rather than reflect how people actually choose between Cheltenham schools.

Where Does the Data Come From?

School names and locations come from the Department for Education's Get Information about Schools (GIAS) register, refreshed monthly. Grid references are converted from the register's Ordnance Survey National Grid coordinates.

FAQs

What Is an InPost Locker?

A self-service parcel locker where you can send and collect parcels at any time it's accessible, using a QR code or the InPost app — no staff interaction needed. Handy for Vinted, eBay and other online marketplace sellers, since most InPost lockers are accessible outside normal shop hours, several of them 24/7.

How Do I Send a Parcel from a Locker?

Book your parcel through the courier or marketplace you're using (InPost is used directly, and by carriers including Evri for locker drop-off), then take the QR code or booking reference to the locker to open a compartment and drop your parcel in.

Where Does This Data Come From?

InPost's own public locker location service, refreshed hourly. Locker availability can change, so it's worth checking the InPost app for live compartment availability before a special trip.

By Train

Cheltenham Spa station is around 2 miles from the ground, roughly a 40-minute walk. From the station, local buses run into the town centre, from where it's a shorter walk or short taxi ride up Prestbury Road to Whaddon Road. A taxi direct from the station takes about 10 minutes.

By Car and Park & Ride

Whaddon Road itself has very limited parking on match days, and nearby residential streets operate parking restrictions. Cheltenham's two Park & Ride sites — Arle Court Transport Hub (GL51 6SY, near M5 Junction 11) and Cheltenham Racecourse (GL50 4SH, seasonal) — run regular buses into the town centre, leaving a walk or short taxi on to the ground; neither runs a direct matchday shuttle to Whaddon Road, so allow extra time on top of the bus journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Is the Council's Financial Year?

  • It runs from 1 April to 31 March, so {{ y.year }} covers {{ y.label }}.{% unless y.complete %} The council has published {{ y.months }} months of this year so far, up to {{ y.last_month | append: "-01" | date: "%B %Y" }}.{% endunless %}

Does This Show Everything the Council Spent?

  • No. It lists payments to suppliers where the charge to one budget is £500 or more, and purchases on the council's payment cards. For the council's spending by service, see the council accounts page.

Do the Amounts Include VAT?

  • The council's files don't say whether amounts include VAT, so the figures are shown exactly as published.

Why Are Some Suppliers Shown as Redacted?

  • The council's files show some payee names as redacted. These payments are left out of the top suppliers, because they can't be put against one supplier, but they are counted in every total.

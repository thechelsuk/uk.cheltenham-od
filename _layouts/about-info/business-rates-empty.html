Frequently Asked Questions

When Does an Empty Business Property Start Paying Rates?

  • After three months empty, or six months for certain industrial properties. From then on the full rates are due, unless the property is exempt. The council's unoccupied properties page explains the rules.

Which Empty Properties Are Exempt?

  • Among others, listed buildings, properties whose rateable value is mostly land, and some properties owned by registered charities or community amateur sports clubs. The table above gives the exemption the council records for each property.

What Does the Start Date Show?

  • The start date on the council's record for the empty property. It is usually when the property became empty or changed hands, so treat it as a guide to how long it has stood empty rather than an exact date.

What Does "Lists Empty" Show?

  • How many of the council's lists since {{ rates.releases.first.name }} showed the property empty. A property empty in every list has stayed empty for most of the year, though a short let between two lists wouldn't show.

About This Data

Data from {{ rates.source }}'s business rates lists, with no licence stated. Rateable values are set by the Valuation Office Agency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Warnings Are Listed?

  • Every Met Office warning that lists Gloucestershire among the areas it covers.
  • A warning for Gloucestershire may cover only part of the county, so open it on the Met Office site to see its map.

Why Aren't Older Warnings Listed?

  • The Met Office feed only shows warnings that are in force or coming up, so this log is built from what it shows each time it is checked. Warnings from before this page started aren't included.

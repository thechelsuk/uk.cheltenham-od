Frequently Asked Questions

What Is a Rateable Value?

  • The Valuation Office Agency's estimate of the yearly rent a property could have been let for on the open market on a set date. For the revaluation that took effect on 1 April 2026, that date was 1 April 2024. It is not a sale price or a rates bill.

How Is a Business Rates Bill Worked Out?

  • The council multiplies the rateable value by a multiplier the Government sets each year, then takes off any relief. From April 2026 properties valued below £51,000 pay a lower small business multiplier, and those valued at £500,000 or more pay a higher one. The council's calculating your business rates page lists each multiplier.

Who Gets Small Business Rate Relief?

  • A business that occupies one property, or one main property plus small extra ones, valued at no more than £15,000. Up to £12,000 the relief is 100%, and it tapers between £12,000 and £15,000. The council's rate relief page has the full rules.

Do Empty Business Properties Pay Rates?

  • Yes, after a while. No rates are due for the first three months a property is empty, or six months for certain industrial properties. After that the full rates are due, unless the property is exempt, for example because it's a listed building. See the empty business properties list for each property's exemption.

Why Aren't Business Owners Named?

  • The council leaves out the names of sole traders and individuals, as their names are personal data. This page leaves out every ratepayer's name, and counts holiday lets without listing or mapping them, as many are in or next to someone's home.

How Often Is This Updated?

  • The council publishes new lists a few times a year. In 2026 it published lists for January, April, June and October. This page checks for a new list every week.

About This Data

Data from {{ rates.source }}'s business rates lists for {{ rates.release.name }}, with no licence stated. Rateable values are set by the Valuation Office Agency. Properties are placed on the map by their postcode using postcodes.io (ONS Postcode Directory). {% include freshness.html file="_data/business-rates.json" %} Rateable values changed for most properties on 1 April 2026, when the 2026 revaluation took effect, so values in earlier lists aren't comparable with current ones. Each circle sits at its postcode's centre, not the property itself.

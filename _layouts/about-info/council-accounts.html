Frequently Asked Questions

What Does Net Spending Mean?

  • It is what a service cost the council after its own income, such as fees, charges and some grants, is taken off. The figures cover the council's day-to-day (revenue) spending, not capital spending such as building projects.

Why Is Highways and Transport Below Zero?

  • A figure below zero means the service's own income was larger than what it spent. Highways and transport includes the council's off-street car parks, which in 2025-26 took in more than they cost.

Why Are Education and Social Care Missing?

  • Cheltenham Borough Council reported no spending under those headings in these years, so they are left out of the table.

How Is This Different From the Payments Pages?

  • The council spending pages list individual payments to suppliers. This page shows the council's own year-end figures for each service, as reported to the government, so the two don't add up to the same totals.

When Is a New Year Added?

  • The government publishes each council's figures some months after the financial year ends. This page shows the latest five years.

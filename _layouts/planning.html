Understanding the References

An application reference beginning with a two-digit year (for example 26/00415/FUL) shows the year it was submitted. FUL is a full application; OUT is an outline application, where only the principle of development is agreed and the detail is reserved for later. Click any Status to open the full record on the council's portal.

FAQs

Which Trains Does This Page Show?

The next departures and arrivals at Cheltenham Spa station, up to 10 of each. Trains that have already left or arrived aren't shown.

How Often Are the Train Times Updated?

Regularly throughout the day, and the time of the latest update is shown above the tables. Delays and platform changes can happen at short notice, so check National Rail for live departures and arrivals before you travel, and the station screens when you arrive at the station.

What Do Scheduled and Expected Mean?

Scheduled is the timetabled time. Expected is when National Rail now expects the train to depart or arrive, which is later than the schedule when a train is running late.

What Does Calling at Mean?

The stations a train stops at after leaving Cheltenham Spa, in order.

Where Does the Train Data Come From?

Train times come from National Rail Enquiries' live departure boards. Passenger figures are estimates from the Office of Rail and Road.

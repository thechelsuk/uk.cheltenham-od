Frequently Asked Questions

What Number Do I Call in an Emergency?

  • Call 999 when someone's life is at risk, someone is badly hurt, or a crime is happening now.
  • For urgent medical help that isn't a 999 emergency, call NHS 111. To report a crime that isn't an emergency, call the police on 101.

Is There a 24-Hour Mental Health Crisis Line in Gloucestershire?

  • Yes. Call 111 and choose option 2 to reach the NHS mental health crisis team run by Gloucestershire Health and Care, at any time of day or night. On a textphone, call 18001 111.
  • You can also call Samaritans free on 116 123 or text SHOUT to 85258, both at any time.

Who Do I Call About a Power Cut?

  • Call 105, free from any phone, or 0800 6783 105. Both put you through to the local electricity network operator. Our power cuts page shows current cuts around Cheltenham.

Who Do I Call About Flooding?

  • It depends on where the water is coming from. For flooding from a river, call the Environment Agency on 0800 80 70 60. For surface water or groundwater, contact Gloucestershire County Council on 01452 425 000. For flooding on a road, call county highways on 08000 514 514, and for a public sewer, Severn Trent on 0800 783 4444.
  • Floodline, on 0345 988 1188, gives flood warnings and advice, and our flood warnings page shows live warnings and river levels.

Does the Council Give Out Sandbags?

  • No. Cheltenham Borough Council doesn't supply sandbags to individual homes. It keeps a small stock to protect places such as hospitals, schools and electricity substations if flooding affects the town.
  • Sandbags, flood boards and air brick covers are sold by most builders' merchants and online.

Are These Numbers Free to Call?

  • Numbers starting 0800 and 0808, and 999, 111, 101, 105 and 116 123, are free from landlines and mobiles.
  • Numbers starting 01, 0300 and 0345 are charged at the standard rate, and are usually included in call bundles. Each table shows the cost of every number.

How Up to Date Are These Numbers?

  • Every number is checked against the provider's own site, and the date of the latest check is shown above. If you find one that's changed, please let us know.

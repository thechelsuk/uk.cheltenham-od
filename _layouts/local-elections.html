Frequently Asked Questions

How Often Are Cheltenham Councillors Elected?

  • Cheltenham Borough Council is elected by halves: one seat in each of the 20 wards is contested every two years. All 40 seats were contested in 2024 because the ward boundaries were redrawn.

How Many Councillors Does Cheltenham Have?

  • Forty, two for each of the town's 20 wards.

Who Is My Local Councillor?

  • Find your ward on the Cheltenham wards page. Each ward profile lists its two councillors, with a link to their page on the council's website.

Where Do These Results Come From?

  • Cheltenham Borough Council's own published election results and councillor pages, checked for consistency and refreshed monthly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Fires Are Counted?

  • Every fire that fire and rescue services recorded on grassland, woodland or crops, from small grass fires to large field and hillside fires.
  • Fires in gardens, parks, bins and buildings are not included.
  • Official statistics do not define a wildfire, so these counts include small fires that would not meet the fire service's definition of one.

What Counts as Cheltenham and the Surrounding Area?

  • Cheltenham is every small census area in the GL50 to GL54 postcodes, which takes in Bishop's Cleeve, Winchcombe and the villages of the north Cotswolds as well as the town.
  • The surrounding area is every other small census area whose centre is within {{ data.radius_km }} km of Cheltenham town centre, such as Churchdown, Brockworth, Badgeworth and the east of Gloucester.

Why Is the Latest Year Missing for Cheltenham?

  • Fire services across England moved to a new national recording system in November 2025. The fire statistics for {{ data.last_year }} were published without the location of any fire in England, so fires for that year cannot be split between Cheltenham and the surrounding area.
  • It is not yet known whether locations will be added in a later release. The {{ data.county }} column covers every year, so the county-wide trend continues either way.

  • {{ totals.deliberate_pct }}% of the fires around Cheltenham since {{ data.first_year }} ({{ totals.deliberate_display }} of {{ totals.all_display }}) were recorded as started deliberately.

Which Fire Severity Days Are Recorded?

  • The Met Office issues a new fire severity forecast each morning. Any day rated high ({{ fsi.level }} of 5), very high or exceptional around Cheltenham is added to the table here.
  • Low and moderate days, which make up most of the year, are not listed.
  • The current forecast for the next five days is on the wildfire risk page.

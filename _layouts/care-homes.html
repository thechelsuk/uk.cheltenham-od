Frequently Asked Questions

What Do CQC Ratings Mean?

  • The Care Quality Commission rates services outstanding, good, requires improvement or inadequate, overall and for each of five questions: are they safe, effective, caring, responsive to people's needs, and well-led?
  • A rating reflects the service at its last inspection, so check the date of the report. Older reports may not reflect how a home is run today.

What's the Difference Between a Residential and a Nursing Home?

  • Residential care homes provide personal care, such as help with washing, dressing and medication. Nursing homes also have qualified nurses on duty around the clock for people with more complex medical needs.

Why Do Some Services Have No Rating?

  • New services, or ones that have recently changed owner, aren't rated until the CQC has inspected them.

Who Pays for a Care Home?

  • It depends on your needs and your savings. Gloucestershire County Council can assess what care you need and whether it will help with the cost; its health and social care pages explain how to ask for an assessment.

Where Does This Data Come From?

  • The Care Quality Commission's directory of registered services and its latest ratings, both published under the Open Government Licence. Each home is placed at the centre of its postcode on the map.

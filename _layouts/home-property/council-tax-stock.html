What Is the Council Tax Stock?

  • A yearly count of every home on the council tax valuation list. It counts homes, not sales or prices.

Why Doesn't It Match Housing Supply?

  • They are different measures from different sources. The stock is a total headcount at one date each year, while housing supply counts the change over a financial year, so the two rarely line up exactly.

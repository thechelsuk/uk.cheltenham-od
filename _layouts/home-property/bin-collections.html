Frequently Asked Questions

How Do I Know if I'm Week 1 or Week 2?

  • Enter your postcode in the council's collection day checker. It lists the dates for your recycling, green bin, food caddy and garden waste. Once you know whether your recycling falls in Week 1 or Week 2, this page shows which week it is.

What Time Should I Put My Bins Out?

  • By 7am on your collection day. In weeks with a bank holiday, some areas are collected from 6am.

Where Can I Take Extra Rubbish or Recycling?

  • Extra bags beside your bin aren't collected. Our recycling page maps the town's recycling banks and household recycling centres, and the council collects large items for a fee.

Why Doesn't This Page Show My Street's Day?

  • The council only publishes collection days through its postcode checker, one address at a time, so we link to it rather than copy it.

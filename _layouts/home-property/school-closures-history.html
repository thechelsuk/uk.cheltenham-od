Frequently Asked Questions

How Far Back Does This History Go?

  • Gloucestershire County Council removes each closure from its page once the day has passed and keeps no archive, so this history starts on {{ history.tracking_since | date: "%-d %B %Y" }}, when we began recording them.
  • Closures are kept here for two years.

Why Might a Closure Be Missing?

  • We check the council's page four times a day. A closure posted and removed between two checks, such as one taken down after a school reopened, would not be recorded.
  • Only closures posted on the council's page are included. A school that told parents directly but didn't post with the council won't appear.

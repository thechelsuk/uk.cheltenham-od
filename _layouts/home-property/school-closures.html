Frequently Asked Questions

Is My Child's School Closed Today?

  • If the school is not in the table above, it has not posted an emergency closure on Gloucestershire County Council's closures page.
  • We check the council's page four times a day, so a closure posted in the last few hours may not show here yet. The council's closures page always has the latest, and your school's own website, app or text messages come first.

Why Do Schools Close in an Emergency?

  • Common reasons are snow and ice, a burst pipe or loss of heating or hot water, a power cut, or not having enough staff to open safely.
  • Each school's head teacher decides whether to close and posts the closure with the council.

Does This Include Half Term and Inset Days?

  • No. Only unplanned closures appear here. For planned holidays, see the council's term dates.

Can I Get an Alert When a School Closes?

  • Yes. Subscribe to our school closures feed in any feed reader, or the all-in-one alerts feed for every local alert. Closures also show at the top of our home page while they are in force.
  • The council also sends email alerts for closures across Gloucestershire.

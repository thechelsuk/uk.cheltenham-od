Frequently Asked Questions

Where Does This Data Come From?

  • National Grid Electricity Distribution's Live Power Cuts dataset — the electricity distribution network operator for Gloucestershire, formerly Western Power Distribution.

What Should I Do in a Power Cut?

  • Call 105, the free 24/7 national power cut number, to report it or check for known outages in your area. Call 999 too if there's a risk to life, such as damage to power lines or a substation.

Why Is the List Sometimes Empty?

  • Most of the time there's no power cut affecting Cheltenham, so the unplanned list and map are empty — that's expected, not a sign the feed has stopped working.

Does This Cover Planned Outages Too?

  • Yes. Planned maintenance work has its own planned power cuts table, with the start and end times, and appears on the map alongside unplanned faults.

How Often Does This Update?

  • The underlying NGED feed updates near real-time; this page and its feed are checked every 5 minutes.

Can I See Past Power Cuts?

  • Yes. Once a power cut against a Cheltenham postcode has been restored, it moves to the power cuts history page, with how many properties it affected.

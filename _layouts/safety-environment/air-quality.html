What Do the Low, Moderate, High and Very High Bands Mean?

  • They follow DEFRA's Daily Air Quality Index thresholds for each pollutant.
  • Each band is worked out from a single reading rather than the official rolling average, so treat it as a guide.
  • When readings are High or Very High, people with asthma, lung or heart conditions are advised to cut down on strenuous outdoor activity.

How Often Are the Readings Updated?

  • This page checks DEFRA's network every two hours and shows the latest reading from each station, with the time it was measured.

Matching Your Site's Colours

Widgets follow each visitor's light or dark mode setting. To always show one theme, add ?theme=light or ?theme=dark to the end of the widget's address, for example {{ "/widgets/house-prices?theme=light" | absolute_url }}.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do the Widgets Cost Anything?

  • No. They are free for any site, personal or commercial, with no sign-up.

How Often Do the Figures Update?

  • Fuel prices update every hour. House prices and rents update each month when HM Land Registry and the Office for National Statistics publish new figures, and affordability updates each year when new earnings figures come out. Your page picks up each change by itself.

Can I Change How a Widget Looks?

  • You can change its height in the code and choose a light or dark theme. Please keep the source credit and the link back to Cheltenham Open Data, which the data licences require.

Do the Widgets Track My Visitors?

  • No. They set no cookies and collect nothing about the people who view them. See our privacy policy.

Can I Suggest Another Widget?

  • Yes. Tell us what local data you would like to show on your site.

Frequently Asked Questions

How Accurate Is a 10-day Forecast?

  • The first three to four days are usually dependable. Accuracy drops off further out, so read days five to ten as a general trend rather than a firm prediction.

How Often Is This Page Updated?

  • Once a day. The "Updated" line under the heading shows the exact time of the most recent refresh.

Where Does the Data Come From?

  • All values are pulled from the OpenWeather API for Cheltenham, Gloucestershire.

What Does "Chance of Rain" Mean?

  • It's the probability that measurable rain falls at some point during the day — not how long it will rain or how heavy it will be. A 40% day means rain is more likely than not to stay away, but keep a jacket handy.

Why Is Wind Shown in m/s?

  • That's the metric OpenWeather returns. To convert to mph, multiply by roughly 2.24 — so 5 m/s is about 11 mph.

What Is the UV Index?

  • A measure of sunburn risk from ultraviolet radiation. 0–2 is low, 3–5 moderate, 6–7 high, 8–10 very high. Consider sun protection from 3 upward.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Roads Does This Page Cover?

  • Local streets and roads looked after by Gloucestershire County Council within {{ data.radius_miles }} miles of Cheltenham town centre, including works by utility companies such as water, gas, electricity and broadband providers.
  • Motorways and major A roads are run by National Highways and are on the roadworks page.

What Is the Difference Between Planned and Unplanned Works?

  • Planned works are booked in advance, from a few days to several months ahead.
  • Unplanned works are urgent or emergency repairs, such as a burst water main or a gas leak, which can start straight away and are listed as soon as they are reported.

Where Does the Data Come From?

  • Street Manager, the Department for Transport's service that councils and utility companies use to plan and record every street works in England.
  • New works and changes are sent to this site as they happen, and the page is updated every two hours.

Why Isn't a Works on My Street Listed?

  • Emergency repairs can be recorded after they start, so they may appear here a little after work begins. For anything not listed, see Gloucestershire County Council's roadworks map.

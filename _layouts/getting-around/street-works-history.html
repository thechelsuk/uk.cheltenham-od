Frequently Asked Questions

What Does This History Include?

  • Every street works on local roads within {{ history.radius_miles }} miles of Cheltenham town centre that has appeared on our street works page since {{ history.tracking_since | date: "%-d %B %Y" }}, whether planned or an emergency repair.

Why Does the History Start in {{ history.tracking_since | date: "%B %Y" }}?

  • It starts when this site began logging street works. It isn't a full archive of works before then.

Why Do Some Works Appear Twice?

  • Rows that look the same are usually separate permits for different parts of the same road. A long job is often split into sections, each with its own permit, and long roads such as Gloucester Road can have unrelated works at each end. Hover over a street name to see its permit number.

What Do the Traffic Management Types Mean?

  • No carriageway incursion: the works stay off the road itself, for example on the pavement or verge.
  • Some carriageway incursion: the works take up part of the road, but traffic can still get past.
  • Give and take: there are no lights or stop/go boards. Drivers from each direction take turns to pass the works, giving way to each other. It's only allowed on short works on roads with a speed limit of 30 mph or less and light traffic.
  • Two-way and multi-way signals: temporary traffic lights, controlling one lane past the works or several approaches at a junction.
  • Stop/go boards: staff on site hold up boards to let each direction through in turn.
  • Lane closure and road closure: a lane, or the whole road, is closed while the works take place.

What About the M5, A40 and A417?

  • Motorways and major A roads are run by National Highways, not the county council. Their planned roadworks are on the roadworks page, with a log of unplanned closures.

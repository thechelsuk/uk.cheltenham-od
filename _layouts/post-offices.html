Good to Know

Opening hours and available services vary from branch to branch, and smaller Post Offices inside newsagents or convenience stores may keep different hours to the shop around them. Before a special trip — for a passport Check & Send, travel money or bill payments — it's worth confirming the branch offers what you need and is open. The official Post Office branch finder lists live opening hours and the services at each branch.

Most branches also handle everyday banking. Personal and business customers of nearly all UK banks can withdraw cash, pay in cash and cheques, and check their balance at any Post Office counter — useful across Cheltenham as high-street bank branches have closed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Counts as a Spill?

  • A spill is one period when a storm overflow's Event Duration Monitor recorded it discharging into a watercourse, from when it started to when it stopped.
  • Each spill is logged here once it has ended, so one that is still running appears on the live overflow status page first.

Does a Spill Mean the River Is Polluted?

  • A spill releases untreated wastewater, diluted by rainwater, so the sewers don't back up during heavy rain. It is a permitted part of how the network copes with storms, and the log records when it happened, not what it did to the water.
  • For measured water quality, see our river water quality page.

Which Overflows Are Included?

  • Every Severn Trent storm overflow within {{ history.radius_miles }} miles of Cheltenham town centre, on the River Chelt and the brooks that feed it.

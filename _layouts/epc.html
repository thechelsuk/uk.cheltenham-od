Frequently Asked Questions

What Is an Energy Performance Certificate?

  • An Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) rates how energy efficient a home is, from A, the most efficient, to G, the least. A home needs one when it is built, sold or let, and each certificate lasts ten years.

What Does C+ Mean?

  • C+ means rated C or better: A, B or C. The C+ column and map show the share of an area's homes with one of those three ratings.

Why Measure Homes Rated C or Better?

  • Band C is the government's benchmark for an energy-efficient home. Its Clean Growth Strategy set the aim of as many homes as possible reaching band C by 2035, where practical, cost-effective and affordable.
  • The government has also confirmed that privately rented homes in England and Wales will need to reach band C by 1 October 2030, so the share of homes already at C or better shows how far an area has to go.

What Does the Median Rating Mean?

  • Line up an area's homes from A to G and the median is the rating of the home in the middle: half the homes are rated the same or better, and half the same or worse. It is the rating of a typical home in the area.
  • Each home counts once, using its latest certificate.

Does This Cover Every Home in Cheltenham?

  • No. It covers the {{ borough.homes_display }} homes that have had a certificate, as homes that haven't been sold, let or built since certificates began don't have one.
  • {{ data.older_than_ten_years_pct }}% of these certificates are more than ten years old, so some homes will have been improved since they were rated.

How Can I Find the Rating for a Particular Home?

  • Search by postcode on GOV.UK's find an energy certificate service.

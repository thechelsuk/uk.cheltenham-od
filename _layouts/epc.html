Frequently Asked Questions

What Is an Energy Performance Certificate?

  • An Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) rates how energy efficient a home is, from A, the most efficient, to G, the least. A home needs one when it is built, sold or let, and each certificate lasts ten years.

How Is the Average Rating Worked Out?

  • Each rating counts as a score, from 1 for G up to 7 for A, and the average of those scores is shown with its nearest letter, so an average of 4.0 is a D and 4.5 or more is a C.
  • Each home counts once, using its latest certificate.

Does This Cover Every Home in Cheltenham?

  • No. It covers the {{ borough.homes_display }} homes that have had a certificate, as homes that haven't been sold, let or built since certificates began don't have one.
  • {{ data.older_than_ten_years_pct }}% of these certificates are more than ten years old, so some homes will have been improved since they were rated.

How Can I Find the Rating for a Particular Home?

  • Search by postcode on GOV.UK's find an energy certificate service.

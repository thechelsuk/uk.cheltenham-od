Frequently Asked Questions

What Does a Sponsorship Look Like?

  • A single tasteful card above the fold on a page — your logo, a short line and a link. Always clearly labelled as sponsored. No pop-ups, no auto-playing anything. Like the advert at the top of this page.

Is There a Limit on the Number of Adverts per Page?

  • Yes, pages are limited to a single advert per page. This reduces noise and clutter and puts your business front and centre alongside the page content, directly in front of a local and engaged person looking for relevant content.

Which Pages Will My Advert Appear On?

  • You sponsor a whole category, so your card shows on every page in that section.
  • We keep adverts off a handful of pure utility pages — contact, about, and privacy.
  • Subject to availability.

Why Do Some Categories Have More Pages Than Others?

  • The site is updated frequently and new pages are added over time, so category sizes shift naturally as content grows — it isn't a fixed number.
  • Some categories are bigger simply because their topic has more closely-related pages, such as yearly archives or filtered views of the same dataset.
  • Categories are kept well-balanced and targeted rather than forced to match — your card only ever appears on pages genuinely relevant to your business.

How Do I Pay?

  • By card through Stripe, billed quarterly. Three-month minimum, then cancel any time.

Can I Change My Card Details or Listing?

  • Yes. You can manage your own payment details through Stripe, and email me any time to update your wording or logo.

Will the Link Help My SEO?

  • Sponsor links are marked rel="sponsored" in line with Google's guidance — it's the honest approach and keeps the site's standing healthy. You will however be visible to your target market audience above the fold on the page that matters.

How Much Traffic Do You Get?

  • The site is new and growing quickly. I'm happy to share current visitor numbers on request — just ask.

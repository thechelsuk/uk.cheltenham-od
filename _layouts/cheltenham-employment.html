FAQs

Why Does the Rate Jump Around so Much Year to Year?

These rates come from the Annual Population Survey, which estimates from a sample of residents rather than a full count. For a town the size of Cheltenham, that sample is small enough that year-to-year swings of several percentage points are normal statistical noise, not necessarily a real change in the local economy — look at the overall trend rather than any single year.

Why Is the Unemployment Rate Missing for Some Years?

The ONS suppresses the unemployment rate for a given year when the underlying sample is too small to be statistically reliable at local authority level. This happens intermittently throughout the series, more often in recent years, and is normal — not specific to Cheltenham.

Where Does This Data Come From?

Nomis, the Office for National Statistics' official service for labour market data, refreshed monthly. See our All About Cheltenham page for the current headline figures, population and Census 2021 statistics.

FAQs

Where Do Gloucestershire Play Their Home Matches?

Most home matches are at the Bristol County Ground, also known as the Seat Unique Stadium, in Bishopston. Each summer some are played at the College Ground in Cheltenham as part of the Cheltenham Cricket Festival.

When Is the Cheltenham Cricket Festival?

It is held each summer. The 2026 festival ran from 8 to 23 August. Gloucestershire Cricket announces the dates for each year's festival, so check its official festival page for the next one.

Do the Women's Team Play in Cheltenham?

Yes. Gloucestershire Women play at the Cheltenham Cricket Festival. In 2026 they played Sussex Sharks on 8 August and Kent on 16 August.

Where Can I Find Fixtures and Buy Tickets?

On the club's own website. Use the fixtures, women's cricket and tickets links above, since dates, formats and ticket prices change from year to year.

How Do I Get to the College Ground?

It is in the grounds of Cheltenham College on the south side of the town, about half a mile from the Promenade and 1.2 miles from Cheltenham Spa railway station. Check the club's festival page for parking and travel advice before you go, and see our train times and car parks pages.

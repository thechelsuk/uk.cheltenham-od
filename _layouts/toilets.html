Good to Know

Facilities vary from site to site — not every listed toilet is staffed, free, or open around the clock. Some require a RADAR key for wheelchair-accessible cubicles, and a few inside shops or venues may only be usable during that venue's own opening hours rather than the times shown here. Where a charge applies, it's shown in the features column, but it's worth carrying change just in case a card reader is out of action.

If you spot a toilet that's closed, missing, or listed incorrectly, you can report it directly to the Great British Toilet Map, which keeps this data up to date through user submissions.

What Is a RADAR Key?

  • A key that opens accessible toilets in the National Key Scheme, so they stay available for disabled people. {{ t_radar.size }} of the toilets here use one, and keys are sold by Disability Rights UK.

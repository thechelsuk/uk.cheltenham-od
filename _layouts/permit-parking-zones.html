Frequently Asked Questions

Which Permit Zone Is My Street In?

  • Find your street in the Streets table above, or click a coloured line on the map to see its zone.
  • Some streets appear in more than one zone, where a zone boundary runs along or across them.
  • If your street isn't listed, it isn't in a residents' permit zone, but other parking restrictions such as yellow lines or limited waiting bays may still apply.

What Does "Part of Street" Mean?

  • Only some of the street's length is inside the zone. Those streets are drawn as dashed lines on the map, near the part covered by the zone.
  • Open the council's zone map in the Zones table to see exactly where the boundary falls.

How Do I Get a Parking Permit?

  • Residents' permits in Cheltenham are issued by Gloucestershire County Council, which manages on-street parking across the county.
  • See the council's permit information pages for eligibility, prices and how to apply.

Is Zone 15 Permanent?

  • Zone 15 was introduced under an experimental traffic order, which lets the council test a scheme and review it before deciding whether to keep it.
  • The council's Zone 15 page has the latest on the review.

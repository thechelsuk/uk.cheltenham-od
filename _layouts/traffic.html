FAQs

What Is a Unique Visitor?

A unique visitor is a single device or browser that visited the site at least once in the 30 days, however many pages it looked at or how often it came back. The daily chart counts each day separately, so someone who returns on several days appears on every one of those days but only once in the headline figure. It counts devices rather than people, so someone who uses a phone and a laptop counts twice.

What Counts as a Request?

Every file the site serves: pages, images, scripts, data files and feeds. One visit to a page usually makes several requests, which is why total requests is much larger than unique visitors.

Do the Figures Include Bots and Search Engines?

Yes. Search engine crawlers, feed readers and other automated tools also request pages, and they are included in the requests and in the unique visitor counts. Some are useful, since they are how people find the site, but the figures will be higher than the number of people reading the site. Bots that Cloudflare blocks before they reach the site are not shown as visitors.

What Does Percent Cached Mean?

Cloudflare keeps copies of the site's files in data centres around the world. This is the share of the data sent to visitors that came from one of those copies rather than from the site's own server. A higher percentage means the site loads quickly for more people and needs less work from the server behind it. Pages and data change through the day, so the figure rises and falls.

Do These Figures Identify Me?

No. These counts come from the service that delivers the site, not from cookies or scripts running in your browser, and they contain no names or personal details. See the privacy policy for more.

How Often Are the Figures Updated?

Every day, and the page always shows the latest 30 complete days. Today is left out until it has finished, so the newest day on the page is yesterday.

Can I Use These Figures When Deciding on Sponsorship?

Yes, that is why the page is public. Bear in mind the points above about bots and repeat visits. If you have questions, get in touch or read more about sponsoring a category.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Counts as an Unplanned Closure?

  • A lane or road on the M5, A417, A40 or A46 closed at short notice rather than for planned roadworks, for example after an incident or a breakdown, including lanes closed using overhead signs.
  • Planned roadworks are listed on the roadworks page.

How Long Are Closures Kept Here?

  • Every unplanned closure within {{ history.radius_miles }} miles of Cheltenham is kept for two years from when it was last seen, newest first.

How Is the End Time Worked Out?

  • National Highways reports when a closure ends. If a closure stops being reported before an end is given, the time it was last seen is used instead.

Can I Get Alerts?

  • Yes. Subscribe to the unplanned closures feed in any RSS or feed reader to see each new closure, and when it ends.

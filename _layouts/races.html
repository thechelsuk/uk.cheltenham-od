Getting to the Racecourse

Cheltenham Racecourse is at Prestbury Park, about two miles north of the town centre. During the Festival there's a dedicated shuttle from the town and Cheltenham Spa railway station, park-and-ride services, and heavy road traffic around the course — so staying central or on the north side of town makes race-day mornings easier. The free light-railway and shuttle links run frequently on race days.

If you arrive at the station, a short walk up the honeybourne line footpath will take you most of the way, take the main roads if you need liquid refreshment or other faciliites en route.

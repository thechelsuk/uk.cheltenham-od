Frequently Asked Questions

How to Read the Map?

  • Each shaded area is an electoral ward. The darker the blue, the higher the share of premises that can get a gigabit-capable connection (1000 Mbps+). Pale wards aren't badly connected — almost everywhere here can get at least superfast broadband — they simply have fewer gigabit-ready lines so far. Click any ward for its full breakdown.

Where Does This Data Come From?

  • Ofcom's Connected Nations programme, which publishes fixed broadband coverage for every UK postcode. Ofcom refreshes it roughly twice a year.

Is This the Speed I'll Actually Get?

  • No — it shows what's available to order, not guaranteed speeds. Your real speed depends on your provider, package, and the line to your home.

What Do the Ward Figures Represent?

  • We take every postcode in GL50–GL54, match it to its ward, and average the results, so each ward shows one representative value. A mostly-fast ward can still contain individual streets that lag behind.

Why Show Wards Instead of Postcodes?

  • There are several thousand postcodes across these areas — too many to read on a map. Wards group them into recognisable neighbourhoods while keeping the full detail in the table above.

How Current Is It?

  • Check the "last updated" date at the top of the page. Because Ofcom publishes about twice a year, very recent rollouts may not appear yet.

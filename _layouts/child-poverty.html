About these figures

These are Department for Work and Pensions estimates of children aged 0 to 15 living in low-income families. Relative low income measures families against the current UK median income; absolute low income compares against a 2010/11 income threshold adjusted for inflation. Before-housing-cost figures use income before rent and other housing costs; after-housing-cost figures deduct those costs. The source publishes after- housing-cost ward figures only for FYE 2024 and 2025 in this release.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Does "Low Income" Mean?

  • Relative low income compares family income with the UK median. Absolute low income compares income with a fixed 2010/11 threshold adjusted for inflation. The figures are reported both before and after housing costs, which can change a family's income position substantially.

Why Are There No After-Housing-Cost Figures for 2022 and 2023?

  • The DWP's current local-area release provides after-housing-cost ward figures for FYE 2024 and FYE 2025. The two before-housing-cost series cover FYE 2022 to FYE 2025.

How Often Are the Figures Updated?

  • The DWP publishes the local-area statistics annually. This page is updated manually when a new workbook is released; figures can be revised in a later edition.

Can These Figures Be Added Up to Get an Exact Town Total?

  • Not exactly. The source rounds counts and applies statistical disclosure control to guard against identifying individuals, so ward counts may not sum to a Cheltenham-wide total.

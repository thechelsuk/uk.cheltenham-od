Frequently Asked Questions

What Is a DPD Pickup Point?

  • A local shop, newsagent or Post Office that's signed up to accept DPD parcel drop-offs and collections on DPD's behalf, in addition to its normal business. Many aren't Post Offices at all — Cheltenham's DPD network includes convenience stores, petrol station kiosks and Argos, several open well beyond typical shop hours. Useful if you're regularly sending items sold on Vinted, eBay or another marketplace.

How Do I Drop off a Parcel?

  • Book your parcel through DPD (or a marketplace/carrier using DPD for delivery), then take your QR code or booking reference to the pickup point during its opening hours to drop it off.

Where Does This Data Come From?

  • DPD's own public pickup point finder, refreshed hourly. Opening hours can change, particularly around holidays, so it's worth checking with the shop directly before a special trip.

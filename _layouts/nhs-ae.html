FAQs

What Does Seen Within Four Hours Mean?

It is the share of A&E attendances where the patient was admitted, transferred or discharged within four hours of arriving.

What Is a 12-Hour Wait From Decision to Admit?

A patient who needed a hospital bed waited 12 hours or more between the decision to admit them and being admitted. The table counts these each month.

Do the Figures Cover Cheltenham General Hospital on Its Own?

No. NHS England publishes the figures for the whole of {{ ae.trust.name }}, which runs Cheltenham General Hospital and Gloucestershire Royal Hospital, so they can't be split by site.

Which Hospitals Have an A&E?

The NHS directory lists an A&E at {{ hospitals.ae_hospitals_display }}. It doesn't record opening hours, so check NHS 111 or the hospital before you travel.

How Often Are the Figures Updated?

NHS England publishes them on the second Thursday of each month, covering the previous month, and this page checks for new figures every day.

Where Does the Data Come From?

The A&E figures come from NHS England's monthly A&E attendances and emergency admissions statistics, and the hospitals from the NHS Organisation Data Service. Both are published under the Open Government Licence.

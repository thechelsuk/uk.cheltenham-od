Food Bank FAQs

How Do I Donate to a Food Bank in Cheltenham?

Open a food bank's page to see what it currently needs, what it has plenty of, and where you can drop donations off, including supermarket collection points and their opening hours.

Where Does This Information Come From?

Give Food, a registered charity that keeps the UK's largest public database of food banks and the items they ask for. Each food bank's page links back to the original entry.

How Often Is It Updated?

Every few hours. Food banks change what they need often, so the date each list last changed is shown next to it.

Is the List of Needed Items Complete?

Treat it as a guide. Food banks often have more detail than a simple list can show, so check the food bank's own shopping list before you buy.

Do I Need a Referral to Get Help From a Food Bank?

Many food banks ask for a referral from a professional such as a GP, school or advice charity. Contact the food bank first to check how it works.

Why Are Some Food Banks Outside Cheltenham Listed?

The table shows every food bank within {{ data.radius_miles }} miles of the town centre, so nearby places such as Gloucester and Tewkesbury are included.

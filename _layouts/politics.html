How Do I Contact My MP?

What Is the Difference Between an MP and a Councillor?

An MP represents the constituency in the House of Commons and works on national laws and issues. Councillors are elected to local councils, such as Cheltenham Borough Council, and make decisions on local services such as planning and waste collection.

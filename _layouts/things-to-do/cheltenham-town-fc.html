Where Do Cheltenham Town Play?

  • At Whaddon Road, Whaddon, Cheltenham, GL52 5NA, their home ground since 1932{% if home_record.venue %}, currently known by its sponsor's name, "{{ home_record.venue }}"{% endif %}.

How Do I Get to Whaddon Road by Train?

  • Cheltenham Spa station is around 2 miles from the ground, roughly a 40-minute walk. A taxi from the station takes about 10 minutes, or local buses run into the town centre, from where it's a shorter walk up Prestbury Road. Our train times page shows the next arrivals and taxi ranks shows where to find a taxi.

Where Can I Park for a Match?

  • Parking at Whaddon Road is very limited on match days. Our car parks page lists the town's car parks with fees and opening hours, and nearby residential streets have parking restrictions, so check the permit parking zones map before parking near the ground.

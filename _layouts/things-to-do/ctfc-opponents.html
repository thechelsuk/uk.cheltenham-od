Frequently Asked Questions

Why Do the Records Start in {{ ctfc.stats_from_display }}?

  • That is the first season with half-time scores, referees and match statistics for every game, so every record on these pages covers the same matches. Cheltenham Town's season-by-season league record goes back to 1999–2000.

Which Matches Are Included?

  • League matches only. Play-offs, the FA Cup, the EFL Cup and the EFL Trophy are not included.

Why Do Chester and Halifax Appear Twice?

  • Cheltenham Town played Chester City and Halifax Town in the Football League, and Chester and FC Halifax Town in the 2015–16 National League. They are different clubs, so each has its own record.

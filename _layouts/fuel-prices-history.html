Frequently Asked Questions

How Are the Daily Prices Worked Out?

  • Each day records the prices at every forecourt within {{ fuel.radius_miles }} miles of Cheltenham as they were last checked that day. Prices are checked every hour, and the last check of the day is the one kept.
  • The cheapest and highest are the lowest and highest prices at any one forecourt. The average is the mean across all of them.
  • A forecourt that has not updated its prices for more than {{ fuel.stale_days }} days is left out, so out-of-date prices do not pull the figures up or down.

Why Does the Number of Forecourts Change?

  • Only forecourts with a current price are counted each day. When a forecourt stops updating its prices, it drops out of the count until it reports again.

What Is the UK Average?

  • The Department for Energy Security and Net Zero publishes average pump prices for the UK each week, taken on Mondays. The charts show each week's figure for every day of that week.

Why Is B10 Not Included?

  • Only one forecourt near Cheltenham sells B10 diesel, so there is no range of prices to compare. Its current price is on the fuel prices page.

How Far Back Does the History Go?

  • To {{ history.first_date }}, when this site started recording local fuel prices. A few days early on have no record. A new day is added every day from now on.

About This Data

Listings are drawn from Historic England's National Heritage List for England, the official register of all nationally protected historic buildings and sites in England. A building's grade reflects its level of special architectural or historic interest, not its condition — Grade I is exceptional national importance, Grade II* is particularly important, and Grade II is special interest. Listing does not by itself grant public access to a building; most entries on this page are private property.

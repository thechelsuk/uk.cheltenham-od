FAQs

What Do the Hygiene Ratings Mean?

The Food Standards Agency scores food businesses from 0 to 5 at inspection: 5 is very good, 4 is good, 3 is generally satisfactory, 2 needs improvement, 1 needs major improvement and 0 needs urgent improvement. The rating covers food hygiene and safety, not the quality of the food or the service.

Which Venues Are Included?

Every food business in Cheltenham that the Food Standards Agency classes as a restaurant, café or canteen, a pub, bar or nightclub, or a takeaway or sandwich shop. Hotels, schools, shops, caterers and mobile food businesses are not listed here, but you can find them on the food hygiene ratings pages.

Why Is My Favourite Place Missing or Awaiting Inspection?

New businesses appear once they are registered with the council, and are marked as awaiting inspection until the council visits. A venue can also be classed under a different type, such as a hotel, in the Food Standards Agency's data.

Why Are Some Venues Not on the Map?

A small number of venues have no location recorded by the Food Standards Agency. They are still in the table.

How Often Is It Updated?

The ratings are refreshed every couple of hours from the Food Standards Agency, so a new rating appears soon after the council publishes it.

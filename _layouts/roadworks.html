Frequently Asked Questions

Which Roads Does This Page Cover?

  • The motorways and major A roads managed by National Highways within {{ data.radius_miles }} miles of Cheltenham, which means the M5, A417, A40 and A46. Roadworks on local streets and side roads aren't included.

How Far Ahead Are Roadworks Listed?

  • Planned works are published about two weeks ahead.
  • Unplanned closures, such as lanes closed after an incident or a breakdown, are listed as they happen and checked every five minutes. For the full live picture on the day, see National Highways travel updates.

Why Isn't My Street Listed?

  • Works on local roads are managed by Gloucestershire County Council and utility companies. See the council's roadworks map for those.

What Does Expected Delay Mean?

  • It is National Highways' own estimate of how much longer a typical journey will take because of the works, for example "Slight (less than 10 mins)" or "Moderate (10 - 30 mins)".

Where Does the Roadworks Data Come From?

  • National Highways publishes its planned roadworks weekly as open data. Works that have finished are removed from this page each day.
  • Unplanned closures come from National Highways' Road and Lane Closures service, which reports closures as they are set, including lanes closed using overhead signs.

## How Busy Is Cheltenham Open Data?

These figures come straight from the service that delivers this site, and cover the most recent 30 complete days. They update every day, so the page is always a rolling month. If you're thinking about [sponsoring a category](/sponsor), this is the audience your business would appear in front of.

Three numbers matter most. **Unique visitors** counts the devices that visited at least once in the month. **Total requests** counts every file the site served, including pages, images, data and feeds. **Percent cached** is the share of the data sent that came from a nearby copy rather than being fetched afresh, which keeps the site fast.

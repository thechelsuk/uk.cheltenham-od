Everything a journalist, blogger or broadcaster needs to write about Cheltenham Open Data. Feel free to quote anything on this page without asking. If you need something that isn't here, [get in touch](/contact).

## In One Line

Cheltenham Open Data is a free, independent hub that pulls public data about Cheltenham and Gloucestershire into one place — fuel prices, crime figures, flood alerts, food banks, GPs, planning, local news and weather to name just a few.

## Boilerplate (Copy and Paste)

### Short (≈25 Words)

> Cheltenham Open Data is a free, independent website bringing together public data for Cheltenham and Gloucestershire — from fuel prices and crime figures to flood alerts, food banks and local news.

### Medium (≈60 Words)

> Cheltenham Open Data is a free, independent website that collects public and open data about Cheltenham and Gloucestershire and presents it in one clear, accessible place. It covers fuel prices, crime figures, flood warnings, food bank needs, GP and pharmacy details, planning applications, recycling, schools, local news and a 10-day weather forecast — all sourced from official providers and updated regularly.

## Key Facts

- **What it is:** A free local open-data hub for Cheltenham and Gloucestershire.
- **Cost to use:** Free, with no paywall.
- **Launched:** August 2026
- **Run by:**  Mat Benfield, an independent resident and developer — [thechels.uk](https://thechels.uk).
- **Funding:** Independent and self-funded, supported by local sponsors, hyper-relevant affiliate links and [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/thechelsuk).
- **Website:** <https://cheltenham-od.uk>

## What We Cover

Live and regularly updated data across:

- Air quality
- Crime data
- EV charging
- Flood warnings
- Food banks (needed items)
- Food hygiene ratings
- Fuel prices
- GPs and pharmacies
- House prices
- Local news and daily summaries
- Planning applications
- Recycling
- Schools
- Security alerts
- Weather (10-day forecast)

Plus classifieds, local events, festivals, points of interest, post offices, public toilets, public holidays, helplines and more.

## Story Angles

A few ways to frame a piece:

- **Cost of living** — free, at-a-glance local fuel prices and house prices.
- **Community resilience** — real-time flood warnings, air quality, security, and live food bank needs lists.
- **Civic transparency** — planning applications and crime data made readable for residents.
- **Independent, local tech** — a self-funded project putting open data to work for one town.

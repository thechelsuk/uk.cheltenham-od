## Community & Support

Local jobs and classifieds, food banks, helplines, healthcare and food hygiene ratings — the everyday support and services that keep Cheltenham running.

Our [classifieds](/cheltenham-classifieds) cover local jobs, services, items for sale and community notices, and [food banks](/cheltenham-foodbank-needed-items) shows what local foodbanks currently need most. If you need support, [helplines and contacts](/cheltenham-helpful-contacts-reports) lists numbers and links, and [GPs and pharmacies](/cheltenham-gp-pharmacies) helps you find a practice near you.

Eating out? [Food hygiene ratings](/cheltenham-food-standards) covers every venue's official Food Standards Agency rating, split into five-star venues, those rated 1 to 4, exempt businesses and ones awaiting inspection. For somewhere to work, meet or just sit, [third spaces](/cheltenham-third-spaces) rounds up Cheltenham's community hubs and casual workspaces.

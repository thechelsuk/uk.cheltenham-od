## Community & Support

Food banks, helplines, healthcare, food hygiene ratings and community spaces — the everyday support and services that keep Cheltenham running.

[Food banks](/cheltenham-foodbank-needed-items) shows what local foodbanks currently need most. If you need support, [helplines and contacts](/cheltenham-helpful-contacts-reports) lists tap-to-call numbers with opening hours and call costs, [local help and support](/cheltenham-helpful-contacts-reports/local-support) points to community groups, places to meet and free or discounted food, [GPs and pharmacies](/cheltenham-gp-pharmacies) helps you find a practice near you with its CQC rating, [care homes](/cheltenham-care-homes) lists every care home, nursing home and home care service with its latest CQC rating, [dentists](/cheltenham-dentists) maps local dental practices and explains how to find an NHS dentist, and [opticians](/cheltenham-opticians) maps local opticians and explains who can get a free NHS sight test. For hospital care, [A&E waiting times](/cheltenham-ae-waiting-times) tracks monthly A&E performance at Gloucestershire Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust, which runs Cheltenham General Hospital, and maps its hospitals nearby, while [COVID-19 in Cheltenham](/cheltenham-covid-19) records the pandemic from 2020 to 2023 through cases, testing, deaths, hospital admissions and vaccine uptake.

Eating out? [Food hygiene ratings](/cheltenham-food-standards) covers every venue's official Food Standards Agency rating, split into five-star venues, those rated 1 to 4, exempt businesses and ones awaiting inspection. For somewhere to work, meet or just sit, [third spaces](/cheltenham-third-spaces) rounds up Cheltenham's community hubs and casual workspaces. Looking to give or volunteer? [Charities](/cheltenham-charities) lists every registered charity based in Cheltenham, with what each one does and its income.

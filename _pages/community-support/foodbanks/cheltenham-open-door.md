## Cheltenham Open Door

Cheltenham Open Door is an independent charity supporting rough sleepers and
vulnerable people, based at St George's Business Park on Alstone Lane. It asks
for food, clothing, toiletries and practical items. This page shows what it
currently needs and where you can drop donations off.

## Christmas 2026 Donations

Cheltenham Open Door is running its annual Christmas appeal. Take donations to
the Guest Hub at Unit 3, St George's Business Park, Alstone Lane, GL51 8HF. Its
Donations Hub in Grosvenor Street is not taking Christmas donations.

| Dates | Days | Hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Until 2026-12-17 | Monday to Thursday | 09:00 to 15:00 |
| Until 2026-12-17 | Sunday | 08:30 to 13:00 |
| 2026-12-18 to 2026-12-26 | Every day | 10:00 to 15:00 |

As well as food and warm clothing, it is filling gift bags for its guests and
says it particularly needs gifts for men. Gifts should be unwrapped, marked as
gifts and kept separate from other donations. It cannot accept alcohol,
medication or anything with a blade (safety razors are fine), and it can't use
smart clothes or footwear, household items, children's toys or bedding. The
full list is in its [Christmas 2026 newsletter](https://cheltenhamopendoor.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Cheltenham-Open-Door-Christmas-26.pdf).

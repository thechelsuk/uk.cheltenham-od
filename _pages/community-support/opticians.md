## Opticians in Cheltenham

Find an optician in Cheltenham. The map and list below show **{{ sites.size }} opticians** in the NHS's directory across the GL50 to GL54 postcodes, with their addresses. That takes in the villages and small towns around Cheltenham as well as the town itself, so check the map before you choose.

Most high-street opticians offer NHS-funded sight tests to people who qualify alongside private tests, glasses and contact lenses. This list can't tell you whether a practice has appointments free, so book with the practice directly, and check whether you are eligible for a [free NHS sight test](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/opticians/free-nhs-eye-tests-and-optical-vouchers/) before you go. The NHS's guide to [visiting an optician](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/opticians/visiting-an-optician/) covers how often to have your eyes tested and what it costs, and you can search for opticians on the NHS's [Find an NHS sight test](https://www.nhs.uk/service-search/find-an-NHS-sight-test/location) service.

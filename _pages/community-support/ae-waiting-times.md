## A&E Waiting Times in Cheltenham

Cheltenham General Hospital is part of Gloucestershire Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust, which also runs Gloucestershire Royal Hospital in Gloucester. This page shows the trust's monthly A&E figures from NHS England: how many people attended, how many were seen within four hours, and how many waited 12 hours or more for a bed. It has three years of monthly history and a map of the trust's hospitals near Cheltenham.

**In an emergency, call 999.** For urgent medical help that isn't an emergency, use [NHS 111](https://111.nhs.uk/). The figures here cover a whole month and are published after it has ended, so they can't tell you how busy A&E is right now.

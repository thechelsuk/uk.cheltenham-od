## Exempt Food Businesses

These businesses are marked as exempt in the Food Standards Agency data and do not receive a standard food hygiene rating.

An exemption generally applies where a business is considered low risk or does not handle open food in the way covered by the standard scheme. Examples can include some businesses selling only wrapped or pre-packed food, and some childminders or community premises. The exact decision depends on the business activity and the local authority's assessment.

Exempt does not mean that food safety rules do not apply. Businesses must still meet the relevant legal requirements, and customers should contact the local authority if they have a food hygiene concern.

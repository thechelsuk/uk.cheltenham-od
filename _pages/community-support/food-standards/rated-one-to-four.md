## One to Four Star Food Businesses

These businesses currently have a food hygiene rating from 1 to 4 in the Food Standards Agency data.

The ratings have different meanings: 4 is good, 3 is generally satisfactory, 2 means improvement is necessary, and 1 means major improvement is necessary. A rating below 5 does not necessarily mean that food is unsafe, but it does indicate that the business had areas to improve when it was inspected.

Ratings are a snapshot of the business at the inspection date. Read the rating date alongside the score, and contact the local authority if you need to report a food hygiene concern.

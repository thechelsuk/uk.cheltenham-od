## Five-star Food Businesses

These businesses currently have a food hygiene rating of 5 in the Food Standards Agency data.

A rating of 5 means the business was found to have very good food hygiene standards at its most recent inspection. The inspection considers food handling, cleanliness, building condition, and the way food safety is managed.

The rating does not judge the taste or quality of the food, the level of service, or whether prices represent good value. Check the rating date because the list reflects the latest published inspection data, not a guarantee about every future visit.

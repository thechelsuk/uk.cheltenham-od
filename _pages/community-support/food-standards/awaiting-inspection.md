## Food Businesses Awaiting Inspection

These businesses are marked as awaiting inspection in the Food Standards Agency data and do not yet have a standard food hygiene rating.

This status usually means that a business has recently registered, started trading, or has not yet received its first inspection. It is not a rating and should not be interpreted as either approval or a failing result.

Once the local authority has completed an inspection, the business may receive a rating or may be recorded as exempt if it falls outside the standard scheme. Customers with immediate food safety concerns should contact [Cheltenham Borough Council's environmental health team](https://www.cheltenham.gov.uk/food-safety).

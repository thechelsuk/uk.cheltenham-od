## The COVID-19 Pandemic in Cheltenham

The first confirmed case among Cheltenham residents was recorded on 4 March 2020. Less than a week later the Cheltenham Festival went ahead, drawing crowds from across the country less than two weeks before the first national lockdown. Over the next four years the town lived through three national lockdowns, the vaccine rollout and repeated waves of infection, with Cheltenham General Hospital and Gloucestershire Royal Hospital caring for thousands of patients with the virus.

This page records that period from February 2020 to December 2023 in figures: confirmed cases and testing among Cheltenham residents, deaths, hospital admissions and occupied beds across Gloucestershire's two main hospitals, and the take-up of the seasonal booster vaccines. The worst week for deaths came in April 2020, during the first wave, while hospital admissions peaked in January 2021 and confirmed cases peaked with the Omicron variant at the turn of 2022.

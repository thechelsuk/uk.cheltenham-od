## Dentists in Cheltenham

Find a dental practice in Cheltenham. The map and list below show **{{ practices.size }} dental practices** in the NHS's directory across the GL50 to GL54 postcodes, with their addresses and how far each is from the town centre. That takes in the villages and small towns around Cheltenham as well as the town itself, so check the distance column before you choose.

This list can't tell you whether a practice is taking new NHS patients, because that changes from week to week and isn't published as open data. Ring a practice and ask before you plan around it, and use the NHS's [Find a dentist](https://www.nhs.uk/service-search/find-a-dentist) service, which is the place to check availability. The NHS's guide to [finding an NHS dentist](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/dentists/how-to-find-an-nhs-dentist/) explains what to do if you can't get an appointment, and [what NHS dental treatment costs](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/dentists/how-much-nhs-dental-treatment-costs/) explains the charges.

New to the area or starting a course? Our [moving to Cheltenham](/moving-to-cheltenham) and [student](/student-guide-to-cheltenham) guides cover registering with a GP too, and [GPs and pharmacies](/cheltenham-gp-pharmacies) lists local surgeries.

## About Cheltenham

Cheltenham is a spa town in Gloucestershire, in the South West of England, known for its Regency architecture, the [Cheltenham Festival](/cheltenham-festival-accommodation) horse racing meeting, and its cluster of [other festivals](/#upcoming-festivals) throughout the year. The town grew up around a mineral spring discovered in the early 18th century, and a royal visit from George III in 1788 helped turn it into a fashionable spa resort — the wide, tree-lined streets and grand terraces around the Promenade and Montpellier date from that Regency-era expansion.

Today Cheltenham's economy leans heavily on technology and cyber security, education, and tourism around its festivals and racing. GCHQ, the government's signals intelligence and cyber security agency, is based on the edge of town and has helped draw a wider cluster of tech and cyber firms to the area, alongside long-established independent schools including Cheltenham College and Cheltenham Ladies' College.

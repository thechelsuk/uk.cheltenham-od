## About Cheltenham

Cheltenham is a spa town in Gloucestershire, in the South West of England, known for its Regency architecture, the Cheltenham Festival horse racing meeting, and its cluster of festivals throughout the year. This page pulls together the town's key population and employment statistics, sourced directly from the Office for National Statistics rather than a third party, and refreshed automatically as new figures are published.

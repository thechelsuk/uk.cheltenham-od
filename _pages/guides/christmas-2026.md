## Christmas in Cheltenham 2026

Cheltenham's Christmas season starts with the lights going on across town on Saturday 21 November and runs through to schools going back on Monday 4 January 2027. This guide brings together the dates that matter, from the switch-on and Christmas market to the school holidays, buses and gritting, with a link to the organiser or official source for each.

Dates and times can change, so check the organiser's page before you set off. Where something hasn't been announced yet, we say so and link to where it will appear.

## Christmas Lights and Switch-Ons

- **Saturday 21 November, from 16:00:** Christmas lights are switched on across the town, according to [Cheltenham BID's Christmas guide](https://cheltenhambid.co.uk/christmas-in-cheltenham/). The Minster turns on its window lights the same evening.
- **Saturday 28 November, 13:00 to 19:00:** the [Christmas Light Switch On at Pittville Pump Room](https://pittvillepumproom.org.uk/christmas2026/), run by The Cheltenham Trust. It's free, with the switch-on between 16:55 and 17:05.
- **3 to 16 December, every evening:** a free Christmas light show projected onto the Municipal Offices on the Promenade, listed in [Cheltenham BID's Christmas guide](https://cheltenhambid.co.uk/christmas-in-cheltenham/).

## Christmas Markets and Late-Night Shopping

- **Friday 27 November, 17:00 to 21:00, and Sunday 29 November, 11:00 to 16:00:** Christmas artisan markets at [Pittville Pump Room](https://pittvillepumproom.org.uk/christmas2026/), both free to enter.
- **Sunday 6 December, 10:30 to 15:00:** [Cambray Christmas Market](https://www.cambray.org/events/cambray-christmas-market/) in Cambray Place, run by Cambray Baptist Church, with community stalls, choirs and Father Christmas.
- **10 to 23 December:** the Cheltenham Christmas Market on the Promenade, with more than 40 chalets and free entry, according to [Cheltenham BID](https://cheltenhambid.co.uk/christmas-in-cheltenham/).
- **Thursday 3 and Thursday 10 December:** late-night shopping, with shops open until 19:00 or 20:00. Council car parks are free from 15:00 on both evenings; Regent Arcade's car park isn't included. Our [car parks page](/cheltenham-car-parks) lists every car park with its fees and hours.

## Carols and Christmas Concerts

- **Saturday 28 November, 11:00:** [Advent Carols with the Minster Singers](https://www.stmatthewsandtheminster.org/minster-singers) at Cheltenham Minster.
- **Sunday 20 December, 15:00:** [A Christmas Cracker](https://cheltenhamtownhall.org.uk/event/a-christmas-cracker/) at Cheltenham Town Hall, hosted by the Mayor of Cheltenham, with the Gloucestershire Symphony Orchestra, local choirs and carols for the audience to join in.
- **Monday 21 December, 19:00:** [Carols for Christmas](https://www.tickettailor.com/events/theorielsingers/2331872) with the Oriel Singers at Holy Apostles Church, London Road.
- **Monday 21 December, 19:30:** [Handel's Messiah](https://cheltenhamtownhall.org.uk/event/messiah-by-g-f-handel/) with Cheltenham Bach Choir at Cheltenham Town Hall.

St Matthew's and the Minster lists its other Christmas services and events on its [Christmas 2026 page](https://www.stmatthewsandtheminster.org/christmasevents2026). For carol services at your own church, check its site or noticeboard. Our weekly newsletter, [The Cheltenham Week Ahead](/newsletter), lists concerts and community events as they're announced.

## Meeting Santa

- **Selected days from 28 November to 23 December:** the [Santa Express](https://www.gwsr.com/whats-on/santa-specials/) on the Gloucestershire Warwickshire Steam Railway. For 2026 every train leaves from Toddington, and every child meets Santa in his grotto and gets a gift.
- **Selected days from 5 to 24 December:** [Christmas Grotto at Webbs Cheltenham](https://www.webbsdirect.co.uk/grotto-cheltenham/), the garden centre's grotto. Children's tickets are £15, including a gift and two activities, and adults and teenagers need a £5 ticket. Book in advance.
- **Sunday 6 December:** Father Christmas visits the [Cambray Christmas Market](https://www.cambray.org/events/cambray-christmas-market/).
- **Monday 14 and Tuesday 15 December:** [Dear Santa](https://cheltenhamtownhall.org.uk/event/dear-santa/), a live children's show at Cheltenham Town Hall, with the chance to meet Santa in his grotto afterwards. Tickets are £19.
- **Friday 18 December, 17:00 to 20:00:** [Christmas Fireworks and Market Stalls](https://ellenboroughpark.com/event/christmas-fireworks-market-stalls/) at Ellenborough Park, Southam Road, with Santa's grotto, reindeer, market stalls and a musical fireworks display at 19:15. Walk-in tickets are £18.63, or £64.39 for a car pass for up to five people, including the booking fee.
- **Sunday 20 December:** [Santa's Afternoon Tea](https://ellenboroughpark.com/event/santas-afternoon-tea/) at Ellenborough Park, sittings 12:30 to 14:00 and 15:00 to 17:00, where children meet Santa and get a gift.
- **Dates on the booking page:** [Santa's Tea Party](https://tickets.eskimojoevents.co.uk/JLP_Cheltenham2026) at John Lewis Cheltenham on the High Street, with story time, a snack and a gift from Santa. Children's tickets are £25 plus a £2.50 booking fee, and adults' are £8 plus 80p. John Lewis says its shop events are for [My John Lewis members](https://www.johnlewis.com/our-shops/events).

For other shops and garden centres, check their own sites for times and booking.

## Racing at Christmas and New Year

Cheltenham Racecourse holds [The Christmas Meeting](https://www.thejockeyclub.co.uk/cheltenham/) on Friday 11 and Saturday 12 December, and New Year's Day racing on Friday 1 January 2027. On New Year's Day, Stagecoach runs a [Raceday Shuttle Bus](https://www.stagecoachbus.com/promos-and-offers/west/stagecoach-raceday-shuttle-bus) from Cheltenham Spa station car park and from stop 8 on Clarence Street, with tickets from £6.50 single or £10 return.

## School Holidays

Gloucestershire County Council sets the [term dates](https://www.gloucestershire.gov.uk/term-dates) for community and voluntary-controlled schools:

- **Last day of the autumn term:** Friday 18 December 2026
- **First day of the spring term:** Monday 4 January 2027
- **February half term:** spring term 3 ends on Friday 12 February 2027, and term 4 starts on Monday 22 February 2027

Academies, free schools, foundation and voluntary-aided schools can set their own dates, and any school can add training days, so check with your child's school. If snow or ice closes a school, it appears on our [school closures page](/cheltenham-schools/closures). Our [schools page](/cheltenham-schools) covers every school in the town.

## Gritting and Icy Roads

Gloucestershire County Council grits the county's roads; its core winter season runs from October to April. According to its [gritting page](https://www.gloucestershire.gov.uk/highways/roads/winter-and-severe-weather/gritting):

- It has 29 gritters, each with a snow plough, and its priority network covers about 28% of the county's roads.
- Priority routes are A and B roads, roads to hospitals and emergency stations, some bus routes, roads to the main shopping centres and roads to most secondary schools. Other roads are treated only in long spells of severe weather, when time and resources allow.
- Decisions are based mainly on road surface temperature, not air temperature, using forecasts and roadside weather stations. Each route takes about three hours to treat, and the aim is to finish before ice forms.
- Pavements and cycle paths aren't treated in advance.

The council's [gritting map](https://gcc.dynamicmaps.co.uk/MapThatPublic/login.html?user=WGrit_public&treeid=55) shows the gritting routes and the county's 4,800-plus grit bins. Report an empty or damaged grit bin on [FixMyStreet](https://fixmystreet.gloucestershire.gov.uk/). Gloucestershire Highways posts updates on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/glosroads) and [X](https://twitter.com/glosroads).

Before a cold snap, check our [10-day forecast](/cheltenham-10-day-weather-forecast) and any current [weather warnings](/cheltenham-10-day-weather-forecast/weather-warnings).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When Are Cheltenham's Christmas Lights Turned On?

- Lights go on across the town from 16:00 on Saturday 21 November 2026. The Pittville Pump Room switch-on is on Saturday 28 November 2026, with the countdown between 16:55 and 17:05.

### When Is the Cheltenham Christmas Market in 2026?

- The Christmas market on the Promenade runs from 10 to 23 December 2026, and entry is free.

### When Do Gloucestershire Schools Break Up for Christmas 2026?

- Schools whose term dates are set by Gloucestershire County Council break up on Friday 18 December 2026 and go back on Monday 4 January 2027. Academies and some other schools set their own dates.

### Is My Road Gritted?

- Check Gloucestershire County Council's [gritting map](https://gcc.dynamicmaps.co.uk/MapThatPublic/login.html?user=WGrit_public&treeid=55). A and B roads, some bus routes, and roads to hospitals and most secondary schools are treated first; most residential streets and pavements aren't treated in advance.

## Cyber Attacks and Online Fraud

Gloucestershire's [Community Risk Register](/cheltenham-at-risk) rates a cyber attack as the most likely of the seven top emergencies facing the county. Attacks on essential services and businesses can disrupt things everyone relies on, while scams aimed at individuals, from fake delivery texts to bogus investments, can empty bank accounts.

Cheltenham has a closer connection to cyber security than most towns: it is home to GCHQ, whose National Cyber Security Centre leads the UK's defence against cyber attacks and publishes free advice for the public.

This page explains who to tell if something goes wrong, how common attacks are according to the government's latest survey, and where to find advice on protecting yourself, your family and your business.

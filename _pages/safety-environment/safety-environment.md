## Safety & Environment

Keep an eye on what's happening locally — crime figures, the River Chelt, air quality, power cuts, security alerts and street issue reports.

Our [crime data](/cheltenham-crime-data) page breaks down monthly street-level figures by neighbourhood, sourced from Police UK, with a full yearly archive back to 2023. How safe are the roads? Our [road collisions](/cheltenham-road-collisions) page counts every police-recorded injury collision since 2021 by severity, road user and road, with a map of the fatal and serious ones. For the River Chelt specifically, we cover three separate angles: [flood warnings](/cheltenham-flood-warnings) and live river levels and rainfall from the Environment Agency, with a [history of past warnings](/cheltenham-flood-warnings/history) an account of the [summer 2007 floods](/cheltenham-flood-warnings/2007-floods) and a [flood zones map](/cheltenham-flood-zones), live [sewage overflow status](/cheltenham-sewage-overflows) from Severn Trent Water's monitors, and [chemical water quality readings](/cheltenham-water-quality) from the Environment Agency's Water Quality Archive.

Beyond the river, [power cuts](/cheltenham-power-cuts) lists electricity outages affecting Cheltenham postcodes, with a [history of past incidents](/cheltenham-power-cuts/history), [air quality](/cheltenham-air-quality) tracks local pollution readings, [security alerts](/cheltenham-security-alerts) shows the current national threat level, and [Fix My Street](/cheltenham-fix-my-street) surfaces reported local street issues like potholes and broken streetlights, with a [history of reports](/cheltenham-fix-my-street/history) by month and category.

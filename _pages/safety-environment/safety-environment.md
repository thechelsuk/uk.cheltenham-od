## Safety & Environment

Keep an eye on what's happening locally — crime figures, flood warnings, air quality readings, security alerts and street issue reports.

Our [crime data](/cheltenham-crime-data) page breaks down monthly street-level figures by neighbourhood, sourced from Police UK, with a full yearly archive back to 2023. For the River Chelt specifically, we cover three separate angles: [flood warnings](/cheltenham-flood-warnings) from the Environment Agency, live [sewage overflow status](/cheltenham-sewage-overflows) from Severn Trent Water's monitors, and [chemical water quality readings](/cheltenham-water-quality) from the Environment Agency's Water Quality Archive.

Beyond the river, [air quality](/cheltenham-air-quality) tracks local pollution readings, [security alerts](/cheltenham-security-alerts) shows the current national threat level, and [Fix My Street](/cheltenham-fix-my-street) surfaces reported local street issues like potholes and broken streetlights.

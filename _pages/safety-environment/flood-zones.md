## Flood Zones in Cheltenham

The map below shows the land around Cheltenham that the Environment Agency says could flood from rivers, including the River Chelt and its brooks. **Flood Zone 3**, in the darker blue, is land with a 1 in 100 (1%) or greater chance of flooding from rivers in any year. **Flood Zone 2**, in the lighter blue, is land with a 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000 (0.1% to 1%) chance. Everywhere else is Flood Zone 1, with a lower chance of river flooding.

The zones show the risk ignoring the effect of flood defences, and they cover flooding from rivers only, not from surface water, drains or groundwater, which caused much of the damage when Cheltenham flooded in 2007. Use the map to get a feel for an area, and the Environment Agency's own [long term flood risk service](https://check-long-term-flood-risk.service.gov.uk/) to check a specific address.

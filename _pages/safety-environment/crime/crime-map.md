## How Crime Compares Across Cheltenham

This map shades every part of Cheltenham by its crime rate: the number of crimes reported to the police over 12 months for every 1,000 people who live there. Using a rate rather than a raw count means a busy, densely populated area can be compared fairly with a quieter one.

Choose between 77 small areas, each home to around 1,500 people, or Cheltenham's 20 electoral wards. Move the slider to step back through earlier 12-month periods and see how each area has changed. Click any area for its figures, or see the tables below the map. For month-by-month totals, see our [crime data](/cheltenham-crime-data) page, and for a fuller picture of each neighbourhood, see the [ward profiles](/cheltenham-wards).

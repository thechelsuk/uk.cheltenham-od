## Grass and Woodland Fires Around Cheltenham

Fire crews are called to grass, woodland and crop fires around Cheltenham most weeks from spring to early autumn. Most are small, but in a dry summer fires on the hills and in fields can spread across more than a hectare. This page counts every grassland, woodland and crop fire recorded in the GL50 to GL54 postcodes and the surrounding area within 10 km of the town centre since April 2010.

Years run from April to March, as in the official fire statistics, so 2022/23 covers April 2022 to March 2023. The hot, dry summer of 2022 saw the most fires in the area so far, and July is the busiest month. For today's risk and the five-day forecast, see the [wildfire risk page](/cheltenham-wildfire-risk).

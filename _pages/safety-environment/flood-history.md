## Flood Warnings History

Every flood warning recorded for Gloucestershire since this page started
tracking them, most recent first, including once each one has been lifted.
This isn't a full historical archive — only warnings seen while this page
has been live. For what's happening right now, see the
[Cheltenham flood warnings page](/cheltenham-flood-warnings).

### The Summer 2007 Floods

Cheltenham's worst modern flooding came before this page started tracking warnings. What happened in June and July 2007, and the record river levels it left behind, is detailed in [The Summer 2007 Floods in Cheltenham](/cheltenham-flood-warnings/2007-floods)

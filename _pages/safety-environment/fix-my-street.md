## About Cheltenham's Fix My Street Reports

This page shows the most recent street and highways problems reported in
Cheltenham and the surrounding Gloucestershire area through
[FixMyStreet](https://www.fixmystreet.com), the independent service run by
mySociety. Each report links directly to the original on FixMyStreet, where you
can see photos, current status and any response from the council.

Reports are grouped into categories such as potholes, trees and vegetation,
footpaths and fly-tipping, and plotted on the map so you can see what is being
reported near you. Reports from earlier weeks are kept on the
[report history page](/cheltenham-fix-my-street/history).

### How to Report a Problem in Cheltenham

To report a new issue, visit [FixMyStreet](https://www.fixmystreet.com), enter
a Cheltenham postcode or street name, and drop a pin on the map at the exact
location. Add a short description and a photo if you can, as this helps the
council identify and prioritise the work.

## Cheltenham at a Glance

Cheltenham is a spa town of around 120,000 people on the edge of the Cotswolds. This page brings together the headline figure from each part of Cheltenham Open Data on one page: who lives here, what homes cost, health care and schools, safety and the environment, getting around, and the town's community and heritage.

Every figure links to the page it comes from, where you can find the full data, how it changes over time and where it comes from. For a fuller picture of the town's history and population, see [About Cheltenham](/about-cheltenham), and to compare the town's 20 wards, see [Cheltenham wards](/cheltenham-wards).

## Cheltenham's Wards

Cheltenham is divided into 20 electoral wards, each represented by councillors on Cheltenham Borough Council. Wards are the neighbourhood-sized areas that the council, the police and the Office for National Statistics all report on, which makes them a practical way to compare one part of town with another, whether you are choosing where to live or just curious how your own patch stacks up.

The map shows every ward, shaded by what homes there have sold for over the latest 12 months. The table beneath sorts the wards by crime reports, house prices, broadband, listed buildings, registered voters, turnout and councillors, and each ward has its own profile with crime broken down by type, house prices over time, election results and the nearest GPs, dentists, opticians, schools, parks, play areas and public toilets.

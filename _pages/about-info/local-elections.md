## Local Elections and Councillors in Cheltenham

Cheltenham Borough Council looks after local services such as planning, waste and recycling, parks and licensing. It has 40 councillors, two for each of the town's 20 [wards](/cheltenham-wards), and they decide how the council spends its budget and sets its priorities.

Since 2002 the council has been elected by halves, which means one seat in every ward, 20 in all, comes up for election every two years. In 2024 all 40 seats were contested at once because the ward boundaries had been redrawn, and results before then are for the old wards, so this page starts from 2024.

Below you will find every sitting councillor, a map showing which party holds each ward, and the result of every borough election since 2024 ward by ward, with turnout and winning margins.

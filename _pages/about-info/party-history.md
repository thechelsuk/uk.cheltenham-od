## General Elections in Cheltenham Since 1918

Cheltenham has elected one MP at every general election since 1918. This page lists each of those elections, showing the winning party, the size of the electorate and the turnout, and how many votes the Conservatives, the Liberals (later the SDP&ndash;Liberal Alliance and the Liberal Democrats), Labour and all other candidates received.

Parties change names and merge over time, so the winning party is shown under the name it used at the time. The FAQs at the bottom of this page explain the changes. For the current MP and a closer look at the most recent elections, see [Cheltenham politics](/cheltenham-politics).

## About & Info

The town itself — population, growth and history, politics, schools, the university, post offices, classifieds, local news and the weather forecast.

[All About Cheltenham](/about-cheltenham) pulls together population, growth and Census 2021 statistics, alongside a full [employment rate history](/cheltenham-employment-history), a year-by-year [earnings history](/cheltenham-earnings-history), an [economy](/cheltenham-economy) page covering jobs, businesses and claimants, a map of every [listed building](/cheltenham-listed-buildings) and scheduled monument in the town, and a map of the land and property owned by [Cheltenham Borough Council](/cheltenham-council-land-and-assets). Wondering who represents the town? [Politics](/cheltenham-politics) shows Cheltenham's current MP and the result of every general election since 2010, and the [party history](/cheltenham-politics/party-history) goes back to 1918. Studying here? The [University of Gloucestershire](/university-of-gloucestershire) page maps its campuses in Cheltenham and Gloucester and shows what is taught at each. If you're choosing a school, [schools & catchment areas](/cheltenham-schools) covers primary, secondary, independent and special schools, with separate [primary](/cheltenham-schools/catchment-areas/primary) and [secondary](/cheltenham-schools/catchment-areas/secondary) catchment maps.

For everyday errands, [post offices & lockers](/cheltenham-post-office-locations) covers Post Office branches, [InPost lockers](/cheltenham-inpost-lockers) and [DPD Pickup points](/cheltenham-dpd-pickup-points) across town. Our [classifieds](/cheltenham-classifieds) cover local jobs, services, items for sale and community notices, and you can [submit a classified advert](/submission) for free. And for keeping up with what's going on, [latest news](/cheltenham-news) aggregates local coverage and [10-day weather](/cheltenham-10-day-weather-forecast) gives you the forecast.

## How Cheltenham and Gloucester Have Grown

This map shows how much of Cheltenham, Gloucester and the surrounding villages was built up in 1975, and how the towns have spread since. Each area is shaded by when it was first built up, from the darkest blue for land already built on by 1975 to the palest for the most recent homes and buildings.

Move the slider to step forward five years at a time. The land built up in the latest five years is outlined, so you can see where new estates appeared on the edges of Cheltenham and Gloucester, and how villages such as Bishop's Cleeve have grown. Click any area to see its postcode district and when it was built up, or compare the districts in the table below the map.

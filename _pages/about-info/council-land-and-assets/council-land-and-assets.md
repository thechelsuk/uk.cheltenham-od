## Cheltenham Council Land & Assets

Every piece of land and property Cheltenham Borough Council reported owning or holding in 2023 — car parks, parks, offices, housing land and more — as required by the [Local Government Transparency Code](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/local-government-transparency-code-2015). Each row is one asset, with its tenure (freehold or leasehold) and how the council holds it.

The council's own open data page for this dataset has since gone offline, so this is sourced from a public [GitHub mirror](https://github.com/chrismytton/cheltenham-council-land-and-assets) of the original council CSV exports, kept by developer Chris Mytton, who also built [an interactive map of the same data](https://www.chrismytton.com/cheltenham-council-land-and-assets/).

A [2021 snapshot](/cheltenham-council-land-and-assets/2021) of the same dataset is also available, using a slightly different set of columns from the council.

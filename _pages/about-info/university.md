## University of Gloucestershire

The University of Gloucestershire teaches from four campuses: Park Campus and Francis Close Hall in Cheltenham, and Oxstalls Campus and City Campus in Gloucester. Pittville Student Village in Cheltenham provides student accommodation, and the university's sports arena is at Oxstalls Sports Park in Gloucester.

Courses are grouped into three academic schools (Arts, Culture and Environment; Business, Computing and Social Sciences; and Education, Health and Science), alongside a Graduate School for postgraduate study and the Countryside and Community Research Institute. Which campus a course is taught at depends on the subject, so check the course page before you travel. The map and tables below show where each campus is, what is taught there and which schools use it.

Cheltenham Spa station is about a 25-minute walk from Park Campus and a 30-minute walk from Francis Close Hall, and it is an 8-minute train journey between Cheltenham and Gloucester. Thinking of studying here? Undergraduate applications go through [UCAS](https://www.ucas.com/explore/unis/aa1a1601/university-of-gloucestershire), and the university's own [how to apply](https://www.glos.ac.uk/courses/apply/) and [open days](https://www.glos.ac.uk/visit-us/open-days/undergraduate-open-days/) pages have the details.

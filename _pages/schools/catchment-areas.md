## Cheltenham Secondary School "Nearest School" Map

This map shades each part of Cheltenham by whichever secondary school sits closest to it in a straight line. It's a quick way to see roughly which school serves an area — but it is **not** an official catchment or priority admission area map, and the shape of a zone here won't match how Gloucestershire County Council actually allocates places.

Gloucestershire doesn't publish fixed catchment boundaries for its secondary schools. Instead, most admissions are decided by measured distance from home to school once a place is oversubscribed, alongside criteria like siblings already attending, faith requirements for some schools, and whether the school is selective. A house a few streets from a school's gates can still miss out to a closer applicant, and a house shown here as nearest to one school may in practice have a real-terms closer alternative once you measure by road rather than as the crow flies.

## Home & Property

Buying, renting or just curious about the area — house prices, planning applications, broadband coverage and recycling all in one place.

Our [house prices](/cheltenham-house-prices) page tracks sales from Land Registry Price Paid Data, with a full year-by-year archive going back to 2023, and our [council tax](/cheltenham-council-tax) page shows charges by band across every parish in the borough. Thinking about an extension or new development nearby? [Planning applications](/cheltenham-planning-applications) are pulled directly from Cheltenham Borough Council's register.

Checking whether an address is well connected is covered by [broadband and mobile coverage](/cheltenham-broadband-internet-speeds), and [recycling](/cheltenham-recycling) covers your nearest bring banks and what's collected locally. Together these give a fuller picture of an area than any single source on its own.

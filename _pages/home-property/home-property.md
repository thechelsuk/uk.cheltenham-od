## Home & Property

Buying, renting or just curious about the area — house prices, council tax, planning applications, connectivity and recycling all in one place.

Our [house prices](/cheltenham-house-prices) page tracks sales from Land Registry Price Paid Data, with a full year-by-year archive going back to 2023. Alongside it, [housing supply](/cheltenham-house-prices/housing-supply) shows net new homes and student accommodation, and the [total number of properties](/cheltenham-house-prices/council-tax-stock) on the council tax list shows how the town's housing stock has grown over time. Our [council tax](/cheltenham-council-tax) page shows charges by band for the borough and the five parishes that set their own precept. Thinking about an extension or new development nearby? [Planning applications](/cheltenham-planning-applications) are pulled directly from Cheltenham Borough Council's register.

Checking whether an address is well connected? [Broadband coverage](/cheltenham-broadband-internet-speeds) is broken down by ward, and [mobile coverage](/cheltenham-mobile-coverage) covers 2G to 5G across all four networks. [Recycling](/cheltenham-recycling) covers your nearest recycling centres and bring banks, what can be recycled, and refill and plastic-free shopping. Together these give a fuller picture of an area than any single source on its own.

## Cheltenham School Closures History

Gloucestershire County Council lists emergency school closures only until the day has passed. This
page keeps a record of every closure posted for a Cheltenham school, so you can look back at which
schools have closed, when, and why, whether for snow, a burst pipe or a power cut.

For schools closed today or in the days ahead, see [Cheltenham school closures today](/cheltenham-schools/closures).

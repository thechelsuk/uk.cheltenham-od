## Cheltenham School Closures

When bad weather or an emergency forces a school to close, the head teacher posts the closure on
[Gloucestershire County Council's closures page]({{ site.data["school-closures"].source_url }}). This page
picks out the Cheltenham schools from that county-wide list, so you can see at a glance whether your
child's school is affected today, or has already announced it will be closed tomorrow.

Snow and ice, burst pipes, loss of heating or hot water, and power cuts are the usual reasons. Every
closure is also kept in the [school closures history](/cheltenham-schools/closures/history), and you
can follow new closures in any feed reader through the [school closures feed]({{ page.feed_url }}).
For every school in town, with Ofsted ratings, see [Cheltenham schools](/cheltenham-schools).

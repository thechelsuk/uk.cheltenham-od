## Cheltenham Housing Supply

How many extra homes Cheltenham has actually gained each year — new builds, conversions and change of use, minus demolitions — going back to 2001.

Alongside general house building, communal accommodation (halls of residence and other shared housing) is tracked separately and split between student and non-student use, giving a rough read on how much purpose-built student housing has been added or lost year to year.

## Council Tax in Cheltenham

Council tax varies by property band (A-H, set by the Valuation Office Agency) and by area — five of Cheltenham's parishes (Charlton Kings, Leckhampton with Warden Hill, Prestbury, Swindon Village and Up Hatherley) set their own additional precept, so pay a little more than the rest of the borough. Most of Cheltenham's ordinary electoral wards fall under "All other parts of Cheltenham" below, since they aren't separately parished.

Not sure which band your property is in? [Check it on GOV.UK]({{ site.data['council-tax'].band_check_url }}) — the same Valuation Office Agency service used to set the figures below.

## Bin Collections in Cheltenham

Cheltenham Borough Council collects household waste on the same day every week, through its contractor Ubico. Food caddies are emptied every week. Recycling and the green general waste bin take turns: one week your green boxes and blue bag go out, the next week your green bin does. The council calls these Week 1 and Week 2, and this page shows which one it is.

Your own collection day, and whether your recycling goes out in Week 1 or Week 2, comes from the council's postcode checker. Below you'll find the coming weeks, what goes in each bin, bank holiday and Christmas changes and the garden waste service. For anything that doesn't fit in your bins, our [recycling](/cheltenham-recycling) page maps the town's recycling banks and centres.

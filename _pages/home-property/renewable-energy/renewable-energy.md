## How Much Renewable Energy Does Cheltenham Have?

Almost all of Cheltenham's renewable electricity comes from solar panels, most of them on the roofs of homes, schools and businesses. A compact town with little open land has almost no room for wind or solar farms of its own, so the larger projects sit in the countryside around it, particularly across the boundary in Tewkesbury borough.

This page shows how many renewable installations there are in Cheltenham, how much they can generate and how that has grown each year since 2014, and how the town compares with the rest of Gloucestershire.

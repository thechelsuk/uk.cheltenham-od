## How Much Energy Do Cheltenham's Homes Use?

Every gas and electricity meter in Great Britain reports how much energy it uses each year, and the government publishes the totals for every council and neighbourhood. This page shows how much gas and electricity Cheltenham uses, how a typical home's use has changed since 2005, and how it compares with the South West and England.

The map shows where in town homes use the most and least. Bigger and older homes, especially detached houses and Regency and Victorian properties that are harder to insulate, tend to use more gas for heating, while flats and newer homes use less. Our [Cheltenham home energy ratings](/cheltenham-energy-ratings) page shows how efficient those same homes are, and [Cheltenham renewable energy](/cheltenham-renewable-energy) shows how much of the town's electricity comes from solar panels.

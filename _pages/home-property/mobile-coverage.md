## Mobile Coverage in Cheltenham

How likely are you to get a usable mobile signal in Cheltenham — at home, out and about, or on a call? This page pulls together Ofcom's official coverage predictions for the Cheltenham constituency, covering every generation of mobile network from old-fashioned 2G through to 5G Standalone, and breaking each one down by whether you're indoors, outdoors, or just somewhere in the local area.

Cheltenham does well by national standards: basic 2G, 3G and 4G voice and data coverage from at least one network is effectively universal here, and even indoors — normally the hardest test, since walls and modern insulation both block signal — 4G reaches almost everywhere. 5G tells a more mixed story: the newer, faster "5G Standalone" technology in particular still has real gaps once you look for a strong ("very high confidence") signal outdoors.

## Unlike Home Broadband, This Isn't Street-by-Street

Ofcom's fixed broadband data (see our [broadband coverage page](/cheltenham-broadband-internet-speeds)) is published postcode by postcode, so it can be mapped ward by ward. Mobile coverage isn't published at that resolution — Ofcom's open data only goes down to constituency level, so every figure on this page is a single average for the whole of the Cheltenham constituency, not a specific street or postcode. Signal at your actual address will depend on how close you are to a mast and what's in the way (see the FAQs below) — this page tells you the town-wide picture, not a guarantee for your exact doorstep.

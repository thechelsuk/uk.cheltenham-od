## Street Works on Local Roads Around Cheltenham

Many hold-ups on Cheltenham's own streets come from street works: water, gas, electricity and broadband companies digging up the road, and the county council resurfacing and repairing it. This page lists the street works on local roads within 6 miles of the town centre, from planned works booked weeks ahead to emergency repairs such as burst water mains, with the traffic lights or road closures each one needs.

Use the map to see where works are, or sort the table by street, start date or distance from the town centre. For the M5, A417, A40 and A46, which are run by National Highways rather than the council, see our [roadworks page](/cheltenham-roadworks). If you're heading into town, our [car parks](/cheltenham-car-parks) page shows where to park, and [bus data](/cheltenham-bus-data) shows any bus service disruptions.

## How Fuel Prices Have Changed Around Cheltenham

Pump prices around Cheltenham change from week to week, and the gap between the cheapest and most expensive forecourts can be 30p a litre or more. This page records the cheapest, average and highest prices for unleaded and diesel at every forecourt within 20 miles of Cheltenham, once a day, alongside the UK average published each week by the government.

Use it to see whether prices are rising or falling, how local prices compare with the rest of the country, and how wide the spread is between forecourts. For today's prices at every forecourt, with a map and the cheapest highlighted, see the [fuel prices page](/cheltenham-fuel-prices).

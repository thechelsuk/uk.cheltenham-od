## Rail Closures at Cheltenham Spa

When engineering work closes the line through Cheltenham Spa, no trains call at the station and rail replacement buses run instead. This page lists each of those closures once it has ended, most recent first, with when the buses started and stopped and the message the station showed at the time.

It isn't a full historical archive, only closures seen since tracking started on 17 October 2026.

## Roadworks near Cheltenham

Planning a journey in or out of Cheltenham? This page lists the planned roadworks and closures on the main roads around the town: the M5, the A417 including the Air Balloon roundabout, the A40 towards Gloucester and the A46 at Shurdington. Each entry shows when the works start and finish, the expected delay and, where one is given, the diversion route. Unplanned closures, such as lanes shut after a breakdown, are listed separately as they happen and checked every five minutes, with a [log of past unplanned closures](/cheltenham-roadworks/unplanned-closures) and a feed you can subscribe to.

Use the map to see where the works are, or sort the table by start date, end date or distance from the town centre. Many of the biggest closures on the A417 are part of the [A417 Missing Link](https://nationalhighways.co.uk/roads-and-travel/road-projects/south-west/a417-missing-link/) scheme, and overnight closures often move from one stretch of road to the next, so it is worth checking again before each trip.

This page covers National Highways roads only. For works on local streets, see Gloucestershire County Council's [roadworks map](https://www.gloucestershire.gov.uk/roads/roadworks), and if a closure affects your route to work or school, our [bus data](/cheltenham-bus-data) page shows current service disruptions.

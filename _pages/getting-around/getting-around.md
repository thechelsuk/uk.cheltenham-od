## Getting Around

Whether you're driving, charging an EV, catching a bus or a train or getting about by bike, this is the local data for getting around Cheltenham.

If you're filling up, our [fuel prices](/cheltenham-fuel-prices) page compares petrol and diesel across every forecourt reporting locally, checked hourly. Driving an EV? Our [EV charging](/cheltenham-ev-charging) page maps every public charge point. Parking in town is covered on our [car parks](/cheltenham-car-parks) page, with fees, charging hours and max stay where recorded, and our [roadworks](/cheltenham-roadworks) page lists planned closures and delays on the M5, A417, A40 and A46 before you set off.

Getting around without a car is covered too: [bus routes and disruptions](/cheltenham-bus-data) are pulled from the official Bus Open Data Service, with disruption reports refreshed every ten minutes and a [history of past disruptions](/cheltenham-bus-data/history), and our [train times](/cheltenham-train-times) page lists the next departures and arrivals at Cheltenham Spa station. Our [cycle routes](/cheltenham-cycle-routes) page maps dedicated tracks and on-road lanes including the Honeybourne Line and NCN 41, along with local pump tracks and bike parks. Most of it is sourced from official government and open data feeds, with car parks and cycle routes drawn from OpenStreetMap, and all of it is refreshed automatically rather than left to go stale.

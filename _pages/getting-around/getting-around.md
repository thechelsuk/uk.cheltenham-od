## Getting Around

Whether you're driving, charging an EV, catching the bus or just need a decent internet connection, this is the local data for getting around Cheltenham and staying connected.

If you're filling up, our [fuel prices](/cheltenham-fuel-prices) page compares petrol and diesel across every forecourt reporting locally, refreshed daily. Driving an EV? Our [EV charging](/cheltenham-ev-charging) page maps every public charge point. Parking in town is covered on our [car parks](/cheltenham-car-parks) page, with fees, charging hours and max stay where recorded.

Getting around without a car is covered too: [bus routes and disruptions](/cheltenham-bus-data) are pulled from the official Bus Open Data Service, and our [public toilets](/cheltenham-public-toilets) map helps with the practical side of a day out. All of it is sourced from official government and open data feeds, refreshed automatically rather than left to go stale.

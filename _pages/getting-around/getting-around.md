## Getting Around

Whether you're driving, charging an EV, catching a bus or a train or just need a decent internet connection, this is the local data for getting around Cheltenham and staying connected.

If you're filling up, our [fuel prices](/cheltenham-fuel-prices) page compares petrol and diesel across every forecourt reporting locally, refreshed daily. Driving an EV? Our [EV charging](/cheltenham-ev-charging) page maps every public charge point. Parking in town is covered on our [car parks](/cheltenham-car-parks) page, with fees, charging hours and max stay where recorded, and our [roadworks](/cheltenham-roadworks) page lists planned closures and delays on the M5, A417, A40 and A46 before you set off.

Getting around without a car is covered too: [bus routes and disruptions](/cheltenham-bus-data) are pulled from the official Bus Open Data Service, with a [history of past disruptions](/cheltenham-bus-data/history), and our [train times](/cheltenham-train-times) page lists the next departures and arrivals at Cheltenham Spa station. Our [cycle routes](/cheltenham-cycle-routes) page maps dedicated tracks and on-road lanes including the Honeybourne Line and NCN 41. All of it is sourced from official government and open data feeds, refreshed automatically rather than left to go stale.

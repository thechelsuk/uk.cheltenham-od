## Cycle Routes in Cheltenham

A map of cycle tracks and on-road cycle lanes in and around Cheltenham, sourced from OpenStreetMap — the same map data that powers most cycle-routing apps. Most of what's mapped is ordinary street-level provision: a track alongside a road, or a marked lane on it. A couple of routes are notable enough to name: the **Honeybourne Line**, a traffic-free path along the old railway towards Bishop's Cleeve, and **National Cycle Network Route 41**, which passes through town on its way between Gloucester and Bishop's Cleeve.

Coverage is only as good as OpenStreetMap's own mapping for this area — dense in some streets, thin in others. The "mapped distance" figures below only count the portion of each route that falls within a few miles of Cheltenham centre, not the route's full length.

## Residents' Parking in Cheltenham

Much of central Cheltenham is covered by residents' permit parking zones, run by Gloucestershire County Council. Inside a zone, on-street spaces are reserved for permit holders during the hours shown on the signs, so it's worth knowing where the zones start and end before you park or move house.

The map colours each zone's streets, and the table lists every street with its zone so you can find yours quickly. Streets marked "Part of street" are only partly inside the zone and are shown as dashed lines. For those, check the council's zone map or the signs on the street.

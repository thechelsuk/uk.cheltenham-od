## Things to Do

Events, festivals, sport, somewhere to eat, things to see, somewhere to stay and days out with the kids — everything for spending time in Cheltenham.

Our [weekly newsletter](/newsletter) rounds up what's on each week, and [festivals](/festivals) covers the town's year-round calendar of Literature, Jazz, Science, Music and racing. For regular activity, [sport & clubs](/cheltenham-sports) covers Cheltenham Town FC, Gloucestershire Cricket, parkrun and local running, cycling, hiking and climbing clubs, with dedicated pages for [Cheltenham Town FC fixtures and results](/cheltenham-sports/cheltenham-town-fc), [Gloucestershire cricket and the Cheltenham Cricket Festival](/cheltenham-sports/gloucestershire-cricket) and [local parkrun events](/cheltenham-sports/parkrun).

Looking for somewhere to eat? [Where to eat](/cheltenham-where-to-eat) maps Cheltenham's restaurants, cafés, pubs and takeaways with their official food hygiene ratings. Got little ones to entertain? Our [play parks](/cheltenham-play-parks) page maps children's playgrounds within 10 miles of the town centre, and our [public toilets](/cheltenham-public-toilets) map shows the nearest facilities, including accessible and baby-changing ones. Visiting or hosting visitors? [Points of interest](/cheltenham-points-of-interest) maps the town's notable sites, and [places to stay](/staying-in-cheltenham) lists local hotels and guest houses. [Public holidays](/cheltenham-public-holidays) rounds out the practical side of planning ahead. Just after somewhere to sit, work or meet? See [third spaces](/cheltenham-third-spaces).

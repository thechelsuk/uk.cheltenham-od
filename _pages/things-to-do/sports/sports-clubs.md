## Join a Sports Club in Cheltenham

Whether you want to race, keep fit or simply find a friendly group to train with, Cheltenham has a club for it. Some are among the oldest in the country: Cheltenham Archers date back to 1857, Cheltenham Rugby Club to 1889, Cheltenham & County Harriers to 1880 and Cheltenham North Rugby Club to 1905. Most welcome beginners and have junior sections, and many offer a free taster session before you commit to membership.

The list covers running and athletics, team sports such as cricket and rugby, and individual sports from archery and boxing to croquet and triathlon, and cycling, climbing, cheerleading and walking groups. For Cheltenham Town FC, Gloucestershire Cricket and parkrun, see our main [Cheltenham sports page](/cheltenham-sports).

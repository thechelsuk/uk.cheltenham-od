## Parkrun in Cheltenham

Parkrun organises free, weekly, timed 5k runs (and 2k junior runs for under-14s) in parks and open spaces across the world, every Saturday and Sunday morning. They're open to everyone, whatever your pace — walkers, joggers and runners all take part, and there's no need to have run before.

Cheltenham has four parkrun events: two 5k Saturday morning runs and two Sunday morning junior 2k runs. All you need is a free parkrun registration and a barcode — printed or on your phone — to get timed.

- [Register with parkrun](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/register/) — free, one-off, works at any parkrun event worldwide.

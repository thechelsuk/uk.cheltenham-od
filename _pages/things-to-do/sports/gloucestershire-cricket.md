## Gloucestershire Cricket

Gloucestershire County Cricket Club, founded in 1870, is the county's professional cricket club, with both a men's and a women's team. Most of its home matches are played in Bristol, but every summer the club brings cricket to Cheltenham for the Cheltenham Cricket Festival, held at the College Ground in the grounds of Cheltenham College.

The College Ground has hosted first-class cricket since 1872, and the festival is described as the longest-running cricket festival on an outfield in the world. W. G. Grace, the club's first captain, scored 318 not out there in 1876, the first triple century in a county match. Gilbert Jessop, one of the game's fastest scorers and captain of the club from 1900, was born in Cheltenham, and his birthplace is on our [points of interest](/cheltenham-points-of-interest) map.

## Gloucestershire Men

The men's team plays in the County Championship, the county game's first-class competition played over four days, as well as the one-day and T20 Blast competitions. The 2026 festival included both a four-day match and shorter-format games.

## Gloucestershire Women

Gloucestershire Women play in the women's domestic one-day and T20 competitions. The women play matches at the Cheltenham Cricket Festival as well as in Bristol, so the festival is a chance to see both teams in one summer.

## The Cheltenham Cricket Festival

The 2026 festival ran from 8 to 23 August at Cheltenham College, with two women's matches and three men's matches, including a four-day County Championship match against Worcestershire. Gloucestershire Cricket announces the dates for each year's festival, so check its official festival page for the next one.

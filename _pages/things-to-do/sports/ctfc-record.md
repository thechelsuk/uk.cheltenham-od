## Cheltenham Town's League Seasons

Cheltenham Town have spent every season since 1999 in the Football League apart from one, 2015–16, when they won the National League at the first attempt. This table sets each season side by side, from their best finishes to their relegation battles, with points per game so seasons of different lengths can be compared fairly.

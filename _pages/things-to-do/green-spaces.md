## Parks and Green Spaces in Cheltenham

Cheltenham's green spaces range from Pittville Park, Montpellier Gardens and Imperial Gardens to neighbourhood playing fields, allotments, bowling greens and quiet churchyards. This page maps every one of them inside the borough, drawn to its real outline, so you can see what is on your doorstep and how far each ward's green space stretches.

The map shades each site by type: parks and gardens, sports grounds and playing fields, play spaces, allotments, and cemeteries and churchyards. Click a site to see its size and how many ways in it has, or use the tables below to compare wards and find the largest spaces. Each ward's own page, such as [Pittville](/cheltenham-wards/pittville) or [Oakley](/cheltenham-wards/oakley), lists the parks in and around it, and the [Cheltenham wards](/cheltenham-wards) page compares them all.

Heading out with children? Our [play parks](/cheltenham-play-parks) page lists every named playground with directions. For a run, [Cheltenham parkrun](/cheltenham-sports/parkrun) is held in Pittville Park, and the [cycle routes](/cheltenham-cycle-routes) map shows how to reach them by bike.

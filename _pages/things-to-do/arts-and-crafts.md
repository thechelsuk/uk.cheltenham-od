## Arts and Crafts in Cheltenham

Cheltenham has a long creative tradition, and today it shows in its working studios, hands-on classes and public galleries. Whether you want to learn to throw a pot, meet local artists in their own studios or simply spend an afternoon looking at great art, you will find somewhere to start below. Nature in Art and The Wilson are both public museums, and the Montpellier quarter is home to a cluster of independent galleries within a few minutes' walk of each other, and Cheltenham Open Studios has grown from a biennial event into a year-round community of local artists.

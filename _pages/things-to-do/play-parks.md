## Play Parks in Cheltenham

Looking for somewhere for the children to burn off some energy? This page maps the play parks and playgrounds in and around Cheltenham: neighbourhood play areas on housing estates, playgrounds inside the Regency parks such as Montpellier Gardens, Sandford Park and Pittville, and further-flung ones across the wider area up to 10 miles from the town centre.

Every play area is shown on the map and in the table below, with its distance from the town centre and a map pin for directions. Click a marker on the map to see its name and a directions link, or sort the table by name or distance to find the nearest one to you.

The council publishes its own [find your nearest play area map](https://maps.cheltenham.gov.uk/map/Aurora.svc/run?script=%5CAurora%5CCBC-sports.AuroraScript%24&resize=always&workflow_id=FindNearestplay), which is worth a look for council-run sites. For rainy days, our [things to do](/explore/things-to-do) guide has more ideas, and the [public toilets](/cheltenham-public-toilets) map helps with the practical side of a day out with little ones.
